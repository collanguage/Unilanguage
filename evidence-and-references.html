Site-wide Research Convention · 全站研究规范

Evidence & References

证据与引文

Rule

Readers must be able to tell what an external source establishes and what Unilanguage proposes.

原则

读者必须能够判断：哪些内容由外部来源确立，哪些内容由 Unilanguage 提出。

01

Two Required Evidence Layers · 两个必须分开的证据层

External established evidence · 外部已确立证据

Traceable sources

Etymological dictionaries, historical dictionaries, historical linguistics research, corpora and primary sources. Factual claims that depend on these sources receive inline numbered citations such as [1].

包括词源词典、历史词典、历史语言学研究、语料库与原始文献。依赖来源的事实性主张使用 [1] 等正文编号引文。

Unilanguage Analysis / Proposed Mapping · Unilanguage 分析／拟议映射

Project interpretation

Cross-language resemblance, phonetic–semantic association, cognitive comparison or proposed protocol mapping. Label it explicitly; never present it as established etymology merely because an external source supports a nearby fact.

包括跨语言相似、语音—语义关联、认知比较或拟议协议映射。必须明确标注；不能因为外部来源支持邻近事实，就把拟议映射写成已确立词源。

03

Source Quality and Claim Placement · 来源质量与主张位置

Prefer primary and authoritative sources · 优先原始与权威来源

Use primary texts, historical dictionaries and scholarly publications for important historical claims. General dictionaries and collaborative references may assist navigation but should not be the sole support for a consequential claim.

Cite where the claim appears · 在主张处引用

Place [1], [2] beside the relevant factual sentence, then provide complete bibliographic details in the References list. A detached list of links is not enough.

Record uncertainty · 记录不确定性

Mark disputed, tentative or limited evidence, and keep counterevidence visible.

Preserve the chain · 保留证据链

Source → Observation → Mapping → Hypothesis → Experiment → Evaluation → Protocol
