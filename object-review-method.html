Seven review targets · 七类审核对象

A · Mapping

One primary Chinese mapping; secondary mappings are independent. Standard equivalence and author proposal must not share an identity.

B · Author rationale

Preserve why the author proposed the mapping without converting the reason into an external fact.

C · Etymology

Check every historical link with dictionary or scholarly evidence. Similarity cannot fill a missing historical step.

D · Hypothesis

Require accurate phonetic categories, testable scope, evidence, counterevidence and calibrated confidence.

E · Other note

Leave material Needs Classification when a reliable category cannot yet be assigned.

F · Experiment

Require an actually executed test, explicit condition/result/status and a reference back to the tested hypothesis.

G · Reviews

Source Verification and AI Review are signed separately for each object and never inherited from the word record.

AI Review decision rule · AI 审核决策规则
