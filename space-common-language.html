Space Common Language Initiative
航天共通语言计划

Carrying human languages and cultures beyond Earth · 让人类语言与文化走向太空

Founding Declaration v1.0 · 创始宣言 v1.0

To enable humanity to carry its diverse languages and cultures into space, while developing a shared capacity for mutual understanding.

让人类带着各自的语言与文化走向太空，同时拥有共同理解彼此的能力。

Original vision: Jinkai Liu · 2026-10-26

From Learning to Space · 从语言学习到航天共通语言

Learn languages → Discover cultures → Share understanding → Accumulate shared semantic knowledge → Explore a space common language.

We seek common understanding without replacing linguistic diversity.

学习语言 → 发现文化 → 分享理解 → 积累共同语义知识 → 探索航天共通语言。

我们追求共同理解，而不是用一种语言取代多样性。

Research Boundary · 研究边界

This initiative is exploratory. It is not a validated operational language or a certified safety-critical communication standard. Any proposed symbols or protocols require evaluation with users, domain specialists and existing aerospace standards before operational use.

本计划属于探索性研究，尚不是经过验证的实际航天工作语言，也不是经过认证的安全关键通信标准。任何拟议符号或协议在实际使用前，都需要与使用者、领域专家及现有航天标准进行比较和验证。
