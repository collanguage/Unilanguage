# Production Worker v1.1

Default method for new tasks generated by Scheduler v0.1. This is a research
instruction/output contract, not an autonomous model service. No new batch is
authorized by installing it. Preserve original observations and author/exposure
identity. Never read private benchmark/holdout packages.

## Lexical–Diachronic task

Source Identity → Source Diachrony → Source Stage Meaning Freeze → Chinese
Candidate Discovery → Chinese Diachrony → Stage-to-Stage Mapping → Phonetic
Comparison → Evidence / Counterevidence → Featured Summary → Depth
Classification → Editorial Freeze Proposal.

Featured Mapping is a reader-facing summary, not the research object.

1. Confirm form, language, grammatical/lexical identity, meaning/context and
   provenance before discovery. Unresolved identity remains gated, not inferred
   from a desired Chinese match. Observation and AI result are separate fields.
2. Source minimum attempt: historical form, language, period, documented sense,
   pronunciation when available, coexisting senses and change/chronology status.
   Missing evidence is explicit Pending, not a demand for unlimited searching.
3. Meaning-first generation: at most 3–5 serious candidates per object, plus
   necessary controls. Expansion is retrieval only. Record generated/retained/
   not selected/semantic mismatch/evidence failure separately.
4. For every serious Chinese candidate attempt modern meaning, historical senses,
   reliable historical pronunciation/reconstruction, and character history or
   phonetic series when relevant. Mainland-first. Record dialect scope if used.
   Use separate candidate/form-labelled stage nodes; the six-layer coverage
   summary must disclose which candidates lack evidence. Unavailable paper
   edition/page, reading or attestation stays visibly Pending.
5. Use the research record contract in pilot-001/proposal.json: source_stages,
   chinese_stages, chinese_coverage, mappings, relations, remaining_pending,
   visible_pending, featured and lexical_subclaim_depth_proposal. Each mapping
   links actual source/Chinese stages and records scope, separate Semantic/
   Phonetic/Structural Fit, evidence, counterevidence and Historical Relation.
   Pending/None found/Not selected may have a null Chinese reference.
6. Distinguish attested coexistence, documented semantic development, editorial
   structural comparison and unknown chronology. Never turn a list into an
   attested arrow. Verified family evidence can be reused; conclusions cannot.
7. Optional Featured is Candidate, with scope and mapping_ref to its deeper
   comparison. It covers only that sense/stage. Low phonetic fit stays Low even
   with strong semantic/structural value. Historical Relation defaults to
   Not claimed; any independent historical-origin claim needs separate review.
8. Use D1–D5 per STANDARD.md. D2/D4 are valid candidate states; D1 is maturity,
   never a quota. For a proposed D1, d1_gate must reference both
   source_historical_stage_ref and chinese_historical_stage_ref, with referenced
   attested historical nodes (evidence_layer=historical), a supporting mapping
   and bilateral_evidence_review. The checker tests declarations/references,
   not whether a source actually proves them. Editorial review remains required.

## Cultural–Structural–Literary task

Keep Standard Meaning, actual structural/cultural/literary layers, controls and
evidence boundaries. D4 does not need invented bilateral etymology. Explicitly
declare has_lexical_historical_claims; actual lexical claims must carry the same
depth_record contract, without changing the object's primary pipeline.

## Output and review

Use scripts/production-worker.mjs validateWorkerOutput and the synthetic example
tests/fixtures/worker-v1.1-lexical.json for the output envelope. The fixture is
not evidence and must never enter the Corpus. Include candidate_id, source_identity,
author_observation (or explicit unknown), ai_research_result, primary_pipeline,
featured_summary, depth_status, visible_pending, evidence_boundaries and
depth_record where applicable. Featured summary references the mapping's
independent fits; it does not create a second rating.

Run:

    node scripts/production-worker.mjs PATH_TO_OUTPUT.json
    pnpm validate
    pnpm test

Stop at Editorial Freeze Proposal / Jinkai Liu Review. No corpus writeback,
Reviewed, Published, holdout release, attribution change or deployment.
Acceptance of research is separate from acceptance of a corpus upgrade.

The Scheduler selection algorithm and batch size are unchanged. New schedules
carry worker_method_version=1.1 and worker_contract per selected task. Legacy
audit replay alone uses workerMethod=null; do not use that compatibility path
to generate new tasks. No prior frozen audit is rewritten.
