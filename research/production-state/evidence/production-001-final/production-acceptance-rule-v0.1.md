# Production Acceptance Rule v0.1

Status: frozen proposal for Jinkai Liu review; applies to future candidate intake, not automatic publication. No schema extension.

1. Research complete ≠ Featured required.
2. Featured Pending can enter canonical candidate dataset.
3. Archive preserves provenance; archive ≠ delete.
4. Author Observation ≠ Evidence. Verbatim original, imported editorial expansion and AI result remain separate.
5. AI Candidate ≠ Evidence. Dictionary scope, semantic/phonetic/structural fit and confidence are independent.
6. Canonical Candidate Entry ≠ Published/Proven Mapping.

## Intake gate

Require an identified source language/form/POS and a bounded sense; justified necessary historical stages (or an explicit unresolved status); preserved provenance and attribution; independent fit dimensions; existing candidates plus controls/rejections; sourced evidence with Pending limits; one primary pipeline; and no unsupported cross-language historical claim.

Featured is optional. Unverified paper pages or deeper etymology need not block a clearly supported modern-sense candidate, provided those stronger claims are explicitly excluded. Missing source identity or evidence for the core selected sense does block intake and sends the record to Research Queue or Archive. Never make up a root or choose a Featured to pass a gate.

Allowed terminal decisions: Ready for canonical candidate entry; Ready with Featured Pending; Archive. If core evidence remains unresolved, record a Research Queue hold, not a false Ready. Status is an editorial decision, not a numerical evidence grade.

Entry/review status, Featured status, Evidence status and Publication status stay independent. Historical Relation = Not claimed absent separate historical-linguistic evidence. Group hits do not raise evidence, literature does not prove etymology, and an entry does not validate an experiment.

Schema validation checks structure and references. Evidence/editorial review checks claim boundaries. Passing either is not publication permission. A reviewer-approved future intake must still check duplicate canonical identity, provenance links, independent evidence dimensions and publication status. This freeze does not create records in the canonical dataset.

Production-exposed items retain Benchmark-ineligible after exposure. Never retroactively turn them into fresh holdouts. Benchmark remains Designed / Frozen / Execution Pending Isolated Evaluator.

## Archive / reopening

Keep original wording, source location/hash, prior editorial interpretation, controls, why archived and what missing evidence would permit reopening. Reopen only on substantive source/identity evidence or explicit author clarification, not a batch-size target. GAN specifically requires a real source form/language/reading/sense; its sound string alone is insufficient.
