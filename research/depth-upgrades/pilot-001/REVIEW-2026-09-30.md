## Chronology and layer decision

- **Attested coexistence:** use only for units actually supported together at the stated source layer; Latin CONVENIR and OE LONG are the main examples.
- **Documented development:** no new strict semantic transition is certified in this pass. A source-form borrowing sequence is not itself a semantic-change sequence.
- **Structural parallel:** motion/affect, shape/configuration, intermediary functions, extent/duration and source/origin are compared at specified nodes, without equal dates or common origin.
- **Chronology unknown:** retain visible gaps; do not draw an unsupported A→B→C. Neither a blank mapping nor Pending forces another character.
