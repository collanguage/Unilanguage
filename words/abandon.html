À BANDON

at someone’s power/disposal — DISPOSAL RELATION

The phrase places something at another’s disposal; this is a relation of authority, not simply the act of handling it. Old French a bandon and Anglo-French a bandun are source forms; À is a normalized display.

短语表示将某物置于他人支配／任其处置的关系，不只是处理动作。古法语 a bandon、盎格鲁法语 a bandun 为来源形式；À 为规范化展示。

Sources · 来源：CNRTL/TLFi: abandon, etymology and history · Merriam-Webster Dictionary: abandon

ABANDONNER

GIVE OVER / leave to another’s disposal; RELEASE / LET GO; LEAVE

The display abbreviates a family including the noun abandon/abandun. CNRTL derives the verb from abandon and records release of a rein around 1100 and leaving entirely from the 12th century. These overlapping senses prevent treating TRANSFER then RELEASE as a proven chronological ladder.

ABANDON

GIVE UP / LEAVE / DESERT

Middle English abandounen, attested in the 14th century, was borrowed through Anglo-French abanduner. Modern meanings include relinquishing an interest and withdrawing support. The later English noun is separate.

Meaning first｜先定语义

Bandon means POWER/AUTHORITY; disposal belongs to the relational phrase à bandon. Modern 办/辦 means handle/manage, prepare or deal with. 说文新附 glosses 辦 as exert effort (致力), not authority. Thus bandon ↔ 办 is a limited control/handling association: Low semantic fit for POWER/AUTHORITY, Medium only for a contextual handling/disposal comparison. It is not an exact root translation. 权柄 is the stronger meaning-first support for authority regardless of its sound.

bandon 的核心是 POWER/AUTHORITY；任其处置关系属于 à bandon。现代办／辦为处理、办理、筹备等；《说文新附》释辦为致力，不是权力。因此 bandon ↔ 办仅保留为支配／办理的有限联系：对权力本义语义适配 Low，只有在处理／处置语境比较时为 Medium，不是词根的精确译词。权柄是更合适的权力义支持，其声音不决定选择。

Meaning first, consonant second.｜先定语义，再验辅音。

Semantic Path studies how one word’s historical meanings develop. Consonant Group Mapping compares an English/French historical word or root with the pronunciation of a semantically selected Chinese candidate. These are independent analyses. The b-p-m-f, d-t-n-l, g-k-h and z-c-s groups predict cross-language sound correspondences, not membership of every Chinese word in one path.

Semantic Path 研究一个词的历史语义怎样发展。Consonant Group Mapping 比较英／法历史词或词根与按语义选出的汉语候选词读音。两者独立；b-p-m-f、d-t-n-l、g-k-h、z-c-s 等组预测跨语言声音对应，不要求汉语路径中的每个字属于同组。

  1. Identify the historical English/French word or root.

    ① 确定英／法历史词或词根。

  2. Verify its historical semantic meaning and phrase boundaries.

    ② 核验历史语义与短语边界。

  3. Select the best Chinese character/word by meaning independently of sound; retain alternatives.

    ③ 先按语义独立选择最合适的汉字／词，保留替代候选。

  4. Only then compare the English/French consonant with the Chinese pronunciation.

    ④ 再比较英／法辅音与汉语读音。

  5. Check the predicted b-p-m-f / d-t-n-l / g-k-h / z-c-s correspondence without requiring a same-group Chinese chain.

    ⑤ 检查是否落入预测辅音组，不要求汉语路径全链同组。

  6. Score semantics, consonant-group fit, phonetic evidence and historical relation independently.

    ⑥ 独立评分：语义、辅音组适配、音值证据与历史关系。

English ban / French ban · 独立词史与词义

English ban (verb) continues Old English bannan, a Germanic proclamation/command verb; Middle English prohibitive senses reflect Norse influence, with later French/Latin influence. The English noun partly derives from the verb and partly from Anglo-French. French ban /bɑ̃/ is a masculine noun of Old Low Franconian origin: public proclamation, feudal summons/jurisdiction, banns, and exclusion in expressions such as mettre au ban. It is not the ordinary French verb “to prohibit” (interdire); the old note’s French ban v. 禁止 is corrected. This distinction does not cancel the independently proposed English ban ↔ 甭 candidate.

英语动词 ban 承自古英语 bannan，属于日耳曼语宣告／命令词族；中古英语禁止等义受古诺斯语影响，后又受法语／拉丁语影响。英语名词部分来自动词，部分借自盎格鲁法语。法语 ban /bɑ̃/ 为阳性名词，源于古低地法兰克语，涉及公告、封建征召／管辖、婚姻公告及 mettre au ban 等排斥用语；它不是通常表示“禁止”的法语动词（interdire）。旧笔记“法语 ban v. 禁止”已纠正，这不取消独立的英语 ban ↔ 甭 候选。

Sources · 来源：Merriam-Webster: ban · Académie française, 9th edition: ban

banal · Feudal use to commonplace · 从封建用法到平常义

English banal is borrowed from French (Merriam-Webster: first known English use 1825). CNRTL traces French banal to feudal ban plus -al, with bannel attested in 1247 and the very common/unoriginal sense in 1798. Merriam-Webster describes a Medieval Latin bannalis route for Old French bannel. Both accounts connect seigneurial jurisdiction and shared-use facilities with the later commonplace sense. Neither derives it from Chinese. Ban and banal are separately evaluated observations, but their related European histories must be deduplicated in any family-based experiment.

英语 banal 借自法语（Merriam-Webster 记英语首见1825年）。CNRTL 将法语 banal 分析为封建法 ban 加 -al，记1247年 bannel 及1798年的极普通／无新意义；Merriam-Webster 则通过中古拉丁语 bannalis 解释古法语 bannel。两者均联系领主管辖、共同使用设施与后来的平常义，不从汉语倒推词史。ban 和 banal 的观察独立评价，但欧洲词史相关，实验按词源家族去重。

abandonner [abandonner] v. 抛弃

a.ban.don.ner

ban v. 禁止。可以用甭beng这个字代表。

donner v. 送。用捅tong字对应，给谁的意思，捅钱。

漢語

甭【beng】v. 用法语ban禁止对应，英语ban禁止对应。

旁【pang】n. 旁边的旁字变成使动的用法，放到一旁，抛弃。与法语ban禁止对应，英语ban禁止对应。这里给一个可能性而已。

捅【tong】v. 给的意思。与法语donner给字对应，与英语donate给予对应。

动【dong】v.

备注：为什么这里分开ban而不是band，因为这个词从法语来，法语里面没有band这个词，是bande包扎。法语中有ban禁止，interdict禁止。法语donner，给的意思。合起来就是禁止给=放弃。如果仅仅从英语角度也可以有这样的解释：a- [a] pref. 没有的意思，用罢ba字对应。band [band] n. 帮伙。不过仅仅是记忆方便这样说，因为这个词从法语而来最后还是从法语角度介绍为好。现在考虑abadon这个词

Basic Meaning · 基本词义

give up; leave or withdraw support, depending on context

放弃／抛弃／离弃；依语境亦译遗弃

English: abandon · Chinese: 放弃／抛弃／离弃 · Français: abandonner.

The English noun in “with abandon” concerns unrestrained behaviour; it is a distinct use, not the verb’s default Chinese translation. · 英语名词 with abandon 涉及无拘束的行为，须与这里的动词义分开。
