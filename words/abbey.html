Original Author Note · 原始作者笔记

英语：abbey ['æbi] n. 修道院。法语：abbaye n. 修道院；abbé n. 修道院院长；abbesse n. 女修道院院长。汉语候选：比 bi；笔 bi；以及文学候选‘蓓 bei／蓓蕾’。解释：这个词应该从犹太教的阿比而来。而比或许是用笔 bi 的意思。或者解释成蓓 bei、蓓蕾；这里用一个蓓蕾的蓓字对应，因为修道院是人的灵魂的修持，而生长出绚丽的玫瑰的蓓蕾的圣所。

English abbey ← Anglo-French / Old French abbeie / abaïe (also abeie / abbaye) ← Late / Medieval ecclesiastical Latin abbatia ← Latin abbas ← Greek abbas ← Aramaic abba “father / my father”

ABBA (FATHER) → ABBAS / ABBOT / ABBÉ (SPIRITUAL FATHER / MONASTIC SUPERIOR) → ABBATIA / ABBAYE / ABBEY (office/institution → monastery)

This is a family/semantic summary, not a linear borrowing chain through every slash-separated form. French abbé and English abbot are parallel descendants of the title family. CNRTL distinguishes abbatia as abbatial office/dignity and as monastery. Merriam-Webster labels abbatia Medieval Latin; Etymonline calls it Late Latin. Father is the shared dictionary gloss; my father is Etymonline’s gloss, not a claim that every occurrence has possessive morphology.

这是词族／语义概括，不是所有斜线词形之间依次借入。法语 abbé 与英语 abbot 是称谓词族的分支。CNRTL 区分 abbatia 的院长职务／尊位义与修道院义。Merriam-Webster 标中世纪拉丁语，Etymonline 标晚期拉丁语。father 是共同释义；my father 按 Etymonline 归属，不断言每一用例都具有所有格形态。

Sources: Merriam-Webster Dictionary: abbey · Merriam-Webster: abbot · CNRTL/TLFi: étymologie de abbaye · CNRTL/TLFi: abbé · Dictionnaire de l’Académie française, 9e édition: abbé · Etymonline: abbey / abbot

Original Author Hypothesis

这个词应该从犹太教的阿比而来

Independent Correction · 独立纠错

The family ultimately traces to Aramaic abba via Greek and Latin, with French and English branches. Abbey is an English institution word, not a Jewish-language name or a Jewish title for a pastor.

该词族最终经希腊语和拉丁语追溯至阿拉姆语 abba，形成法语和英语分支。abbey 是英语机构词，不是“犹太语 abbey”或“犹太人称牧师 abbey”。

Chinese Kinship Research Note · 汉语亲属称谓

父 / 爸 / 爹 / 爷 vary by period, region, dialect and register. Current mainland platform entries document their differentiated senses, while an official research-project report reproduces local-gazetteer evidence of 爹 / 爷 / 爸 as regional father terms. This supports diversity, not a universal replacement timeline or an earliest national attestation. Modern dialects are living diachronic evidence windows, not frozen copies of ancient Chinese.

父／爸／爹／爷随时期、地域、方言与语体而异。大陆现行数字辞书条目记录其分层义项，国家社科基金项目报告所引地方志材料还显示爹／爷／爸作为地域父称并存。这支持多样性，不证明全国统一替代时间线或全国最早书证。现代方言是活态历时证据窗口，不是古汉语的冻结复制品。
