Basic Meaning · 基本意义

sky · /skaɪ/ · noun

天空；天 · 名词

English definition

Sky commonly names the apparent expanse above the earth, where the sun, moon, stars, clouds, and weather are seen. In extended use it may represent height, openness, possibility, heaven, or an upper limit.

中文定义

Sky 通常指地面上方看似展开的空间； 太阳、月亮、星辰、云和天气现象都在其中被看见。它的引申义 可以表示高度、开阔、可能性、天界或某种上限。

Concrete meaning · 具体意义

The visible region above an observer.

观察者上方可见的区域。

Extended meaning · 引申意义

A symbol of height, openness, freedom, remoteness, or transcendence.

高度、开阔、自由、遥远或超越的象征。

04

Etymology · 词源

English: sky · 英语：sky

English explanation

English sky entered Middle English from Old Norse ský, “cloud.” Its historical Germanic line is associated with cloud or cloud-cover. The modern sense expanded from what is seen overhead to the whole apparent expanse above.

中文解释

英语 sky 在中古英语时期来自古诺尔斯语 ský，原义为“云”。它在日耳曼语历史系统中 与“云”或“云层覆盖”有关；现代意义则从头顶所见之物 扩展为上方整个看似展开的空间。

French: ciel · 法语：ciel

English explanation

French ciel continues Latin caelum, “sky” or “heaven.” Its history belongs to the Romance language family and is separate from the Germanic history of English sky.

中文解释

法语 ciel 承接拉丁语 caelum，意为“天空”或“天界”。它属于 罗曼语族的历史发展，与英语 sky 的日耳曼语 历史来源不同。

Chinese: 天空、天、昊、盖 · 汉语：天空、天、昊、盖

English explanation

In modern Chinese, 天空 is the ordinary explicit word for the visible sky. 天 has a wider range that may include sky, heaven, day, weather, or the natural order. Early forms of 天 depict a standing person with the head or uppermost part emphasized; its semantic development connects head, top, high, and sky. 昊 is a literary word for the vast sky. 盖 means a cover or canopy; it is included here as a semantic model, not as the historical source of sky.

中文解释

在现代汉语中，天空是表示可见天空的 常用明确词语。天的意义范围更广， 可以指天空、天界、日、天气或自然秩序。 “天”的早期字形以站立的人为基础，并突出头部或最上方 的位置；其语义发展把头顶、上方、高处与天空连接起来。 昊是表示广大天空的文学用词。 盖指覆盖物或穹盖；本页把它作为 语义模型，而不是把它说成 sky 的历史来源。

Evidence boundary: Similar meanings do not prove shared historical ancestry.

证据边界：意义相似不能证明这些词具有共同的历史来源。

Proposed mapping: sky /skaɪ/ ↔ 盖 gài /kaɪ˥˩/

映射假说：sky /skaɪ/ ↔ 盖 gài /kaɪ˥˩/

English interpretation

The proposal treats the visible sky as an immense cover above the observer. It combines a spatial relation—above and encompassing—with a partial sound resemblance between sky and gài.

Mapping Justification · 映射论证

Common theoretical foundation

Core Cognitive–Semantic Layer

Every Language Book entry uses the same core: embodied experience, image schema, spatial relation, conceptual metaphor, semantic extension, and context verification. For sky, the primary embodied act is looking upward into a field experienced as high, wide, distant, and open.

共同理论基础

核心认知语义层

每一篇语言书词条都采用同一个理论主干：身体经验、意象图式、 空间关系、概念隐喻、语义扩展和语境检验。对于 sky，首要身体经验是抬头仰望一个被感知为高、广、 远且开放的空间场。

Method rule: the cognitive–semantic layer is mandatory; cultural and philosophical layers are selected only when they add relevant, supportable insight.

Core Analysis · 核心分析

Phonetic analysis · 语音分析

Sound relation · 语音关系

sky /skaɪ/ and gài /kaɪ˥˩/ share the /aɪ/ sequence. /k/ and /g/ are both velar stops, but voicing, the initial /s/, and Mandarin tone remain unmatched.

sky /skaɪ/ 与 gài /kaɪ˥˩/ 共有 /aɪ/ 音段；/k/ 和 /g/ 都是软腭塞音。但是清浊、 sky 开头的 /s/ 以及汉语声调并不对应。

Embodied cognition · 身体认知

Position and UP · 位置与上方

Sky is normally construed as above an observer. This stable bodily orientation activates the spatial relations POSITION and UP.

天空通常被理解为位于观察者上方。这个稳定的身体方向关系 激活了 POSITION（位置）和 UP（上方）空间规则。

Image schema · 意象图式

Boundary and cover · 边界与覆盖

The horizon makes the visible sky appear to meet the earth, while the dome-like field overhead can be imagined as a canopy or cover.

地平线使可见天空仿佛与大地相接；头顶上呈穹顶状展开的 空间场，则可以被想象成篷顶或覆盖。

Cognitive semantics · 认知语义

Scale and distance · 尺度与距离

The sky is experienced as high, wide, and distant. These scale relations support both ordinary 天空 and the literary vastness of 昊.

天空被体验为高、广、远。这些尺度关系既支持普通词 “天空”，也支持文学词“昊”所表达的广大感。

Conceptual metaphor · 概念隐喻

Metaphorical projection · 隐喻投射

Physical openness may be projected onto freedom and possibility; height may be projected onto ideals, power, or transcendence.

物理空间的开阔可以投射为自由与可能性；高度则可以投射为 理想、力量或超越。

Protocol test · 协议检验

Context verification · 语境检验

A valid translation must test which feature matters in context: visible atmosphere, heaven, weather, literary vastness, or the metaphor of a cover.

有效翻译必须检验语境中真正重要的特征：可见天空、天界、 天气、文学性的广大感，还是覆盖隐喻。

Yijing Semantic Classification · 易经语义分类系统

System definition

Yijing Semantic Classification is an experimental Unilanguage method for mapping a lexical sense in context—not merely a written word—to one or more trigram or hexagram structures. The mapping uses image, position, relation, state, process, and transformation as semantic features.

A word is not forced permanently into one hexagram. Polysemy and changing context may produce a primary hexagram, a secondary hexagram, or a transformation path between structures.

系统定义

易经语义分类系统是 Unilanguage 的一项实验性方法。它所 映射的不是孤立字形，而是特定语境中的词语义项； 分类可以落入一个或多个八卦或六十四卦结构，并以意象、 位置、关系、状态、过程和变化作为语义特征。

一个词不会被永久强制归入唯一一卦。多义性与语境变化可能 产生主要卦象、辅助卦象，或者卦象之间的变化路径。

6A · Textual evidence + semiotic interpretation

Qian as Heaven · 乾为天

The hexagram Qian is formed by six unbroken yang lines and is associated with heaven. Its sequence moves through images of a dragon hidden below, appearing in the field, leaping at the abyss, and finally flying in heaven. “Flying dragon in the heavens” joins vertical position, movement, power, timing, and visibility in one image.

In this Unilanguage interpretation, Qian can serve as an important symbolic starting point for the semantic analysis of sky. This does not mean that the received Yijing states that all sixty-four hexagrams historically derive from Qian alone.

6A · 文本证据 + 符号学解释

乾为天 · Qian as Heaven

乾卦由六条阳爻构成，以天为象。卦爻辞中的龙，从潜藏、 见于田野、或跃在渊，到“飞龙在天”，形成了一条由下而上、 由隐而显的运动路径。“飞龙在天”把垂直位置、运动、力量、 时机和可见性集中在同一个意象之中。

在本页的 Unilanguage 解释中，乾可以作为分析天空语义的 一个重要象征起点；但这不等于说传世《易经》认定六十四卦 在历史或文本生成上全部只由乾卦产生。

乾，元亨利貞。九五，飛龍在天，利見大人。

Qian: originating and penetrating, beneficial and steadfast. Ninth in the fifth place: a flying dragon in the heavens; it is beneficial to see the great person.

乾：元始、亨通、适宜、守正。九五：飞龙在天，利见大人。

6B · Historical text + structural comparison

Gaitian: heaven as a cover · 盖天：天如张盖

The ancient Gaitian model compared heaven to a circular, opened cover and the earth to a square board. The statement “heaven is round like a spread canopy; earth is square like a chessboard” makes cover a formal cosmological model, not merely a casual metaphor.

This strengthens the semantic bridge between sky and 盖: both can organize an overhead field that encompasses what lies below. It supports conceptual mapping, but it still does not prove a historical word relationship.

6B · 历史文本 + 结构比较

盖天：天如张盖 · Gaitian

古代盖天模型把天比作张开的圆盖，把地比作方形棋局。 “天圆如张盖，地方如棋局”使“覆盖”不再只是偶然的修辞， 而成为一种正式的古代宇宙结构模型。

这加强了 sky 与“盖”之间的语义桥梁：二者都可以 组织一个位于上方、笼罩下方事物的空间场。它支持概念映射， 但仍然不能证明两个词存在历史同源关系。

6C · Literary evidence + poetic association

The Song of Chile · 《敕勒歌》

The northern folk song turns the cosmological image into lived landscape: “The sky is like a domed tent, covering the four wilds.” The sky is perceived not as an isolated object but as an encompassing relation between heaven, grassland, wind, animals, and the human observer.

6C · 文学证据 + 诗性联想

《敕勒歌》 · The Song of Chile

北朝民歌把宇宙模型转化为身体可以经历的风景：“天似穹庐， 笼盖四野。”这里的天空不是一个孤立物体，而是连接天穹、 草原、风、牛羊与观察者的笼罩关系。

敕勒川，陰山下。天似穹廬，籠蓋四野。天蒼蒼，野茫茫，風吹草低見牛羊。

By the Yin Mountains lies the Chile plain. The sky is like a domed tent, covering the four wilds. The sky is blue and vast, the steppe boundless; the wind bends the grass and reveals cattle and sheep.

敕勒川，阴山下。天似穹庐，笼盖四野。天苍苍，野茫茫，风吹草低见牛羊。

6D · Core theory connection: embodied cognition

Cloud, earth, and the moving observer · 云、大地与运动中的观察者

From a mountain, a river, or the sea, clouds appear to travel across the sky. At times the moving frame of vision can even make the earth or buildings seem to drift like boats. Human beings do not experience “sky” as geometry alone; they construct it through body position, horizon, motion, distance, memory, and imagination.

The blue sky may therefore be described poetically as our cover: sun by day, moon by night, and seasons changing beneath it.

6D · 核心理论连接：身体认知

云、大地与运动中的观察者 · Embodied observation

站在山上、河边或海上仰望，白云仿佛在天空中游走；视觉 参照系发生变化时，人有时甚至会觉得大地或房屋像舢板与 邮轮一样漂移。人所经验的“天空”并不只是几何空间，而是 由身体位置、地平线、运动、距离、记忆和想象共同构成。

因而，那蓝天也可以在文学中被称作我们的被盖：昼日夜月， 四季在它的下面变换。

6E · Scientific boundary

Perceived sky and physical structure · 感知天空与物理结构

Clouds, atmospheric layers, celestial bodies, and artificial satellites may all appear within what an observer calls “the sky.” Scientifically, however, they do not form one solid cover: the troposphere, stratosphere, ionosphere, outer space, and satellite orbits are different physical regions.

“Cover” is therefore best treated as a perceptual, cultural, and semantic model—not as a literal description of modern atmospheric science.

6E · 科学边界

感知天空与物理结构 · Perceived sky and physical structure

云、大气层、天体和人造卫星，都可能出现在观察者所说的 “天空”之中；但在现代科学中，它们并不共同构成一个实体 穹盖。对流层、平流层、电离层、外层空间和卫星轨道属于 不同的物理区域。

因此，“盖”最适合被理解为感知模型、文化模型和语义模型， 而不是现代大气科学的字面描述。

Conclusion: the mapping is useful as a semantic model, but it remains a hypothesis rather than an etymological fact.

结论：这一映射可以作为语义模型使用，但它仍是假说，而不是词源事实。
