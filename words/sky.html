01

Literary Introduction · 文学引言

English

Standing on a mountain and looking upward, we see white clouds moving across the sky. At moments, the earth itself seems to move, while houses rise and fall like small boats and ocean liners.

Across oceans and great rivers—wherever human beings can travel—we may never visit every part of Mother Earth. Yet we can still glimpse her skin and the places where her bones emerge: the mountains, ridges and valleys.

The blue sky is the cover above us, changing through day and night, sun and moon, and the turning seasons.

中文

站在山上向天空仰望，觉得天上的白云在游走，偶尔也会觉得大地在移动，房屋仿佛此起彼伏的舢板与邮轮。

在海洋与大河，在一切我们能够航行的地方，我们虽然没有走遍大地母亲的每一处，却仍可走马观花，看见地母的肌肤，以及骨骼外露之处——那便是山脉与丘壑。

蓝天是覆盖我们的被盖，昼日夜月，四季变换。

Basic Meaning · 基本意义

Sky · 天空

the visible region above the earth

Heaven · 天

the sky; also a religious, philosophical or cosmological realm

Firmament · 苍穹／穹苍

Cover · 盖

a structure placed over something; an experimental metaphorical mapping for sky

04

Etymology and Character Form · 词源与字形

English sky

English sky entered Middle English through Old Norse ský, which referred to a cloud. Its meaning later expanded from “cloud” to the visible upper region.

Established · 已确立 Historical account to be accompanied by dictionary citations in the research edition.

英语 sky

英语 sky 经古诺尔斯语 ský 进入中古英语；该词原来表示“云”，后来由“云”扩展为人们所见的上方天空。

Established · 已确立 研究版应继续附权威词源辞典来源。

Chinese 天

Early forms of 天 commonly depict a standing human figure with the head or highest point emphasized. The graph came to designate what is highest and, by semantic extension, the firmament above human beings.

汉字“天”

“天”的早期字形通常表现为一个正面站立的人形，并突出人的头部或最高位置。因此，“天”可以表示人的最高处，并进一步引申为人类头顶上方的苍穹。

Evidence boundary · 证据边界： Graphic interpretations vary among scholars and forms. The formal research edition should reproduce dated forms from reliable palaeographic sources. · 不同时期字形与学者解释并不完全一致；正式研究版应采用有年代和出处的可靠古文字材料。

05

Gaitian Cosmology · 盖天宇宙观

English explanation

The Gaitian model was one of the cosmological models of ancient China. It represented heaven as a canopy or vaulted cover extending above the earth.

The sky resembles a domed tent, covering the surrounding fields.

This image from the Northern Dynasties folk song Song of Chile illustrates the cultural metaphor of the sky as a cover.

中文解释

盖天说是中国古代宇宙观之一，以覆盖物说明天地结构。在这种空间想象中，天如张开的伞盖或穹隆，覆盖大地。

天似穹庐，笼盖四野。天苍苍，野茫茫，风吹草低见牛羊。

北朝乐府民歌《敕勒歌》体现了“天空如覆盖物”的文化隐喻。

Modern scientific description

The visible sky involves the atmosphere, the scattering of light, clouds and the appearance of celestial bodies. This scientific account should not be treated as identical to the ancient Gaitian model.

现代科学说明

人所见的天空涉及大气层、光线散射、云层以及天体的视觉呈现。现代科学解释与古代盖天模型属于不同知识体系，不能直接等同。

01 · Discovery · 发现

Observed and proposed relations · 已观察与拟议关系

Sound hypothesis · 语音假说 G / K / H candidate consonant group

/k/ and /g/ are velar stops; their comparison belongs to the proposed G/K/H hypothesis and remains experimental. · /k/ 与 /g/ 同属软腭塞音；二者的比较属于拟议的 G/K/H 假说，状态仍为实验性。

Cross-language sound mapping · 跨语言语音映射 sky /skaɪ/ ↔ 盖 /kaɪ/

The /aɪ/ sequence and velar consonants are comparable, but initial /s/, voicing and Mandarin tone do not correspond. · /aɪ/ 音段与软腭辅音可比较，但词首 /s/、清浊和汉语声调并不对应。

Cognitive schema · 认知图式 ABOVE → COVER

What extends above a viewer can be conceptualized as a cover, canopy or dome. This supports a cognitive association, not historical descent. · 位于观察者上方并延展的事物可被理解为覆盖物、伞盖或穹隆；这支持认知关联，不证明历史传承。

Cultural interpretation · 文化解释 天 · 乾 · 盖天说

天 supplies the Chinese sky/heaven field; 乾 provides an experimental structural classification; 盖天说 records a historical cultural model of heaven as a cover. · “天”提供汉语的天空／天界语义场；“乾”提供实验性结构分类；“盖天说”记录了以覆盖物理解天的历史文化模型。

Historical cognacy · 历史同源状态： Unknown / Unproven · 未知／未证实 No regular sound correspondence, borrowing path or documentary chain currently establishes English sky and Chinese 盖 as cognates. · 目前没有规律音变、借词路径或文献链能够证明英语 sky 与汉语“盖”同源。

02 · Creation · 创造

Literary and metaphorical relations · 文学与隐喻关系

Created relation

“The blue sky is the cover above us” and images such as a tent, roof, shelter or boundary deliberately activate a new relation between SKY and COVER in poetry, narration and translation.

被创造的关系

“蓝天是覆盖我们的被盖”，以及帐幕、屋顶、庇护或边界等意象，在诗歌、叙事与翻译中主动建立 SKY 与 COVER 的新关系。

Creation boundary · 创造边界： A literary or metaphorical relation may be meaningful and reusable, but it is not evidence of historical etymology. · 文学或隐喻关系可以有意义、可复用，但不构成历史词源证据。

07

Yijing Structural Classification · 易经结构分类

English

The sixty-four hexagrams may be studied as a structural symbol system composed of yin and yang lines. Collanguage asks whether their combinations can classify recurring states in nature, society, technology and human life, in a manner comparable—at the level of structural analogy—to a code.

乾 Qian ☰ — candidate structure

heaven, above, motion, creative force and strength

坤 Kun ☷ — contrast structure

earth, below, receptivity, support and containment

中文

六十四卦可以作为一种由阴爻和阳爻组成的结构性符号系统来研究。Collanguage 尝试考察这些组合能否像一种结构编码，对自然、社会、技术及人生变化中反复出现的状态进行分类。

乾卦 ☰——候选结构

天、上方、运行、创造性与刚健

坤卦 ☷——对照结构

地、下方、承载、接受与包容

乾卦原文摘录 · Selected Qian text

乾，元亨利貞。初九，潛龍勿用。九二，見龍在田，利見大人。九三，君子終日乾乾，夕惕若，厲无咎。九四，或躍在淵，无咎。九五，飛龍在天，利見大人。上九，亢龍有悔。用九，見群龍无首，吉。

The text is preserved here as a classical cultural source. A complete scholarly translation and edition citation should be added before formal academic publication. · 此处将原文作为古典文化材料保存；正式学术发表前应补充完整英译和版本出处。

Classification boundary · 分类边界： Sky does not derive from Qian. Qian is used here as an experimental structural and cultural classification model. · Sky 并非来源于乾卦；乾卦在这里是 Sky 的实验性结构分类与文化解释模型。

Translation Guidance · 翻译指导

sky → 天空

Default everyday and scientific contexts.

日常与一般科学语境的默认译法。

sky → 天

Compact, classical, idiomatic or cosmological contexts.

简洁、古典、习语或宇宙观语境。

sky → 苍穹／穹苍

Literary register emphasizing vastness or a vault.

强调辽阔或穹隆意象的文学语体。

sky → 盖／穹盖

Only when the cover metaphor is activated; 盖 alone is not the default lexical translation.

仅在“覆盖物”隐喻被激活时采用；单独的“盖”不是日常默认词义翻译。
