Advance

/ədˈvæns/ · verb / noun

① Literary Introduction · 文学引言

To advance is to leave one position and move toward another. “往” also opens a direction: movement is not merely motion, but motion toward somewhere. · Advance 是离开一个位置，朝另一个位置移动；“往”也打开一个方向：移动不只是运动，而是朝向某处。

② Basic Meaning · 基本词义

English: advance / advancement · French: avancer / avance / avancement · Chinese lexical equivalents: 推进 tuījìn / 前进 qiánjìn

Classification boundary · 分类边界： advance ↔ 往 wǎng is the author-proposed primary research mapping and remains Candidate / Not AI Reviewed. 推进 and 前进 are secondary lexical mappings. The research mapping is not presented as the ordinary dictionary translation or as historical cognacy.

④ Historical Etymology · 历史词源（独立层）

Source-backed chain: English advance ← Middle English advauncen / avauncen ← Anglo-French avancer ← Vulgar Latin *abantiāre ← Latin abante (“before, in front,” from ab + ante).

English advancement reflects Anglo-French avancement, formed from avancer + -ment. French avance is described as a deverbal noun from avancer; French avancement is avancer + -ment.

Boundary · 边界： these sources do not establish a historical morpheme vance meaning “前的,” and they do not support common origin with Chinese 往.

⑥ Mapping Justification · 映射依据

MOVE → FORWARD / TOWARD A DIRECTION

English advance foregrounds movement forward, progress, or causing something to move forward. Chinese 往 profiles movement toward a place or direction. This shared structure motivates comparison, while their lexical ranges are not identical.

英语 advance 突出向前移动、进展或推动；汉语“往”突出朝地点或方向移动。共同结构使比较具有研究价值，但二者词义范围并不完全相同。
