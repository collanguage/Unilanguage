Literary Introduction · 文学引言

English

A short word can name a person, a male adult, or an inherited image of humankind. Across languages, similar sounds may invite comparison; evidence decides how far that comparison may travel.

中文

一个短词可以指人、成年男性，也可以承载关于“人类”的历史观念。跨语言的近音能够发起比较，但能走多远，必须由证据决定。

Etymology / Historical Evidence · 词源／历史证据

English historical record

English man continues Middle English and Old English man / mann / mon, with cognates across Germanic languages. This is an independently documented English/Germanic history.

References: Merriam-Webster: man; Oxford English Dictionary: man.

汉字历史记录

“男”在汉语文字传统中表示男性，并有儿子、爵位等历史义项；“氓”以 méng 表“民、百姓”等义，以 máng 主要见于“流氓”。这些属于各自可追踪的汉语历史与用法。

参考：汉典：男；汉典：氓。

01 · Discovery · 发现

Core case: man /mæn/ ↔ 男 nán

Semantic relation · 语义关系MAN / MALE ↔ 男

The primary senses align closely. Broader historical “person” uses of man should not be projected automatically onto 男. · 核心词义高度对应，但不能把 man 的历史泛指义自动投射到“男”。

Phonetic candidate · 语音候选m-æ-n ↔ n-a-n

Both are compact nasal-bounded syllables with a low/open vowel region. The initial m ↔ n comparison is an experimental Collanguage correspondence, not an established sound law. · 两者都是鼻音边界明显、元音较开放的短音节；词首 m↔n 只是 Collanguage 实验性对应，不是已确立音变规律。

Structural / cognitive relation · 结构／认知关系HUMAN CATEGORY → MALE MEMBER

Both forms can organize a male-human category, while their historical semantic ranges are not identical. · 两者都可组织“人类中的男性”这一范畴，但历史语义范围并不完全相同。

Contextual validity · 语境有效性High in ordinary bilingual glossing

In common noun contexts, man → 男人／男子 is direct; 男 alone may function as noun, adjective-like modifier or category label depending on syntax. · 在普通名词语境中 man→男人／男子很直接；单字“男”可依句法充当名词、修饰语或类别标签。

02 · Supporting Case · 辅助案例

man ↔ 氓 méng / máng

Sound structure · 声音结构/mæn/ ↔ /məŋ/ or /mɑŋ/

Initial m is shared, the vowels occupy a broadly comparable open/central region, and the codas are nasal: n ↔ ŋ. · 词首同为 m，元音大致处于可比较的开放／中央区域，词尾同为鼻音：n↔ŋ。

Semantic layering · 语义分层person / people ↔ common people

The semantic support belongs chiefly to méng “common people,” while the closer modern sound máng belongs chiefly to 流氓. Sound and sense therefore do not converge in one unqualified reading. · 语义支持主要来自 méng“百姓”，更近音的现代读法 máng 主要见于“流氓”；音与义不能在一个不加限定的读音中合并。

Supporting Case only · 仅作辅助案例： useful for testing nasal structure and reading-sensitive semantics; insufficient for a historical or universal rule. · 可用于检验鼻音结构与读音敏感的语义分层，不足以证明历史关系或普遍规律。

Mapping Justification · 映射论证

Why A? · 为什么是 A？Multiple mapping dimensions converge

Core semantics, compact phonetic form, category structure and ordinary bilingual usability converge strongly. · 核心语义、紧凑音形、范畴结构与普通双语可用性多维汇合。

Why not historical certainty? · 为什么不是历史确定性？Different question, different evidence

A historical claim would require dated forms, regular sound correspondences and a credible inheritance or contact path. Those are absent. · 历史主张需要可断代形式、规则音变及可信的继承或接触路径；这些证据目前缺失。

Counterexamples · 反例Many m/n forms are unrelated

Numerous similar syllables lack male/person meanings, and many male/person words lack this sound pattern. A few selected cases cannot establish a universal law. · 大量近似音节不含男性／人义，大量男性／人义词也不具此音形；少量精选案例不能建立普遍规律。

Translation Protocol · 翻译协议

English → Chinese

man → 男人／男子 when adult male is intended; → 人／人类 only when context clearly supports the broader human sense. The Collanguage mapping man ↔ 男 explains structure; it does not override context.

中文 → English

“男”通常按句法译为 male、man、boy 或 son；“氓”应按具体读音与语境译为 common people、subject、ruffian 等，不能因为近音而统一译为 man。
