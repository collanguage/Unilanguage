01

Core Meaning · 核心词义

English · 英语

light /laɪt/ · noun

Light, brightness, illumination; visible electromagnetic radiation and its perceptual, intellectual or spiritual extensions.

本页只讨论“光、光明”义，不讨论另一个历史来源的 light“轻、不重”。

Chinese · 汉语

光 · 光明

The ordinary lexical equivalents of English light are 光 and 光明, not 籁. · 本义层面的标准对应。普通翻译中，English light 通常对应“光／光明”，而不是“籁”。

French · 法语

lumière

法语“光、光明”。它经拉丁语系统发展，与英语 light 在更深层的印欧语系中相关，但不是英语词的直接来源。

Chinese mapping candidate · 汉语映射候选

籁／籟 · lài

本指三孔竹制管乐器，后可指孔窍发出的声音及一般声响，并见于“天籁／万籁”。它不是 light 的标准译词。

02

Primary Mapping · 主要映射

Proposed relation · 拟议关系 light /laɪt/ → 籁 lài /laɪ̯⁵¹/ Mapping Level C — Cross-modal / Semantic · Review Status: Candidate
Phonetic resemblance · 语音相似/laɪ/ ↔ /laɪ/

The modern pronunciations share an approximate sequence; English adds final /t/, while Mandarin has a falling tone and no final /t/. Resemblance is not a regular sound correspondence. · 现代读音共享近似音段；英语多词尾 /t/，普通话有去声且无词尾 /t/。相似不等于规律音变。

Direct lexical semantics · 直接词义Weak · 弱

Light denotes illumination; 籁 historically denotes an instrument and then sound. They are not ordinary lexical equivalents. · light 指光／光明；“籁”历史上指乐器与声音。二者不是日常词义对应。

Cross-modal hypothesis · 跨模态假说LIGHT ↔ SOUND / MUSIC

The literary material repeatedly transforms light, sound, music, inner vision and spiritual illumination, motivating a testable hypothesis. · 文学材料持续在光、声音、音乐、内在视觉和精神照明之间转换，为可检验假说提供动机。

Counterevidence · 反证与限制No cognacy chain · 无同源链

No regular sound correspondence, borrowing path or documentary chain establishes a shared historical origin for light and 籁. · 尚无规律音变、借词路径或文献链证明 light 与“籁”有共同历史来源。

Hypothesis boundary · 假说边界： The mapping does not claim translation equivalence, historical cognacy, or that sound physically becomes light in ordinary perception. · 本映射不主张“light 与 籁互为翻译”、不主张二者历史同源，也不主张声音在普通知觉中会物理地变成光。

03

Historical Etymology · 历史词源

Independent track A · 独立轨道 A

English light

Modern English light ← Middle English ← Old English lēoht / līht ← Proto-Germanic *leuhtam ← PIE *leuk- “light, brightness”.

“光”义的英语 light 由日耳曼语继承，并非借自拉丁语。“轻、不重”义的同形词属于另一历史词根，本词条排除。

Independent track B · 独立轨道 B

French lumière / Latin lumen and lux

French lumière ← Latin luminaria ← luminare / lumen. Latin lux is a related member of the broader *leuk- family, not an intermediate stage in the derivation of lumière.

light, lumière, lumen, lux 表面看似四个 L—光例子，深层去重后集中于一个 PIE *leuk- 词族，不能算作四项独立证据。

Independent track C · 独立轨道 C

籁／籟 lài

“籟”从竹、赖声，《说文》释为“三孔龠也”，本义为古代竹制管乐器；后可指孔穴发出的声音及一般声响，形成“人籁、地籁、天籁、万籁”等表达。

Historical conclusion · 历史结论： English light 与 Chinese 籟／籁之间的历史同源关系 Not established · 未建立。

Cross-modal SOUND ↔ LIGHT · 声音与光的跨模态假说

SOUND → MUSIC → LIGHT → INNER VISION → ILLUMINATION

《如光天籁》 repeatedly presents transformations such as “eye and ear exchanging places,” light rising between musical notes, and a musical staff coloured with light. 一｜音乐：音与光 Music: Sound and Light

中文原文 · Chinese Original

天使之翼合唱团，Song of Life。我们说小孩子们的唱歌的声音像天籁，而光一样是如此，音与光本来就是一家，眼耳互换。那令人爱恋的天使，我也盼望自己，也能生出那天使般的翼，希望是一个天使，愿意因为我的光，而使世界感受爱意。我就是那飞翔的龍，借我的努力传递，聖者的一个美丽与盛达的消息。

English Literary Translation · 英文译文

The Wings of Angels Choir, Song of Life. We say that the singing voices of children resemble the music of heaven; light is like this too. Sound and light belong to one family, eye and ear exchanging places. I long for the beloved angel and hope that I, too, may grow angelic wings—may become an angel whose light lets the world feel love. I am the dragon in flight, carrying through my effort a beautiful and abundant message from the sacred one.

中文原文 · Chinese Original

音乐就是魅力，一种令人欣赏的，一种奖赏。多么迷人的音乐！我们从音中而来，所以我们需要音乐，我们亦回到音乐🎶中。我们柔美的心，那是因为我们从美静的月光如水的古典音乐而来，我们振奋昂扬的心💕，那是从place de arts广场上的节奏有力量的非洲草原上欢狂的跑动而来。记得初中时候听西方古典音乐总是带有一种强烈的想象，好吧就一直想下去，当这些充满着见到的现实中的事物与形象逐渐消泯的时候，那好像与音乐🎶直接而契合的光升起来，透过音符🎶与音符之间的缝隙直接的，就像光透过一个极其细微的孔一下子衍射而明亮并开朗了起来，那是进入了一个自己内在眼睛的世界且无法用言语表达出来的。听到红色娘子军那段常青就义的非常著名的曲目，那简直想到的不是芭蕾舞场景，而是一种似乎是一种宗教的感觉，一种类似于德国十五世纪宗教革命与战争，或许我们中国的整个近代历史的变革就是一种类似近代欧洲的一种变革，一种波澜壮阔的思想的变革，若以极远看，那或许是我们的长江，我们的坨坨河与格拉丹东山，我们的黄河的巴颜喀拉山，山的主人山神换了吧。不论我们承认与否。当然音乐讲法就是各理解个人的。音乐以其独特的魅力，无需言语。Music with its unique charm, without words.

English Literary Translation · 英文译文

Music is enchantment—something to cherish, a kind of reward. What captivating music! We come from sound; therefore we need music, and into music we return. Our tender hearts come from classical music as serene and beautiful as moonlight on water. Our uplifted, impassioned hearts come from the powerful rhythms of dancers running in exultation across an African grassland, here in the Place des Arts. I remember listening to Western classical music in middle school and being seized by intense imagination. Let the imagining continue: as the objects and figures of visible reality gradually dissolved, a light perfectly joined to the music seemed to rise through the spaces between notes. It was like light diffracting through an infinitesimal opening, suddenly becoming bright, spacious and clear—an entrance into the world of one’s inner eye, beyond the reach of words. When I heard the celebrated music of Qionghua’s martyrdom in The Red Detachment of Women, what came to mind was not the ballet scene but something almost religious: the Reformation and wars of fifteenth-century Germany, perhaps, or the transformation of modern China as another immense revolution of thought. Seen from a great distance, perhaps these were our Yangtze and Yellow Rivers, the Tuotuo River and Geladandong, the Bayan Har Mountains—the mountain spirit itself changing. We may acknowledge this or not. Each person understands music in a different way. Music, in its singular power, needs no words.

二｜我们的光明从哪里来 Where Does Our Light Come From?

中文原文 · Chinese Original

我们的光明从哪里来？突然想到，我们现在外面的光明与暗，或许是无数以前以前的那个人他给与的，那么我们自己的光明从哪里来呢？好，那就从我自己现在的修行起来。什么是大圆满。什么是大圆胜慧。这两个名词我近三十年前就知道的，但是不理解，但我一直非常非常喜欢这两个词汇，听到或者看到这两个词汇，就向往着，当然我也不知道具体的怎么回事。总是想通过佛学的学习，道教的基督教印度教等等的学习搞清楚吧！今天我终于搞清楚了。因为我看待这个世界这个宇宙分出了彼此。这是困扰我几十年的难题啊！这是我的从小到大的一种痛苦啊！我无法解决。我今天明白了！过去二十年前，一位我的朋友说我会悟出东西来，看来我是想明白了呀！那天想安静一会，关上我的洗手间的门，那是一片黑暗。突然想到，我们现在外面的光明与暗，或许是无数以前以前的那个人他给与的，那么我们自己的光明从哪里来呢？好，那就从我自己现在的修行起来。我与我的几位顾客谈起我的这个突然的想法，他们也似乎有某种感悟。今天我自己通过我的一个写作突然的想明白了！我想不是佛救我，是我自己救度我自己。那光明本自存在，本自具有，不假外物。或许就是重复曾经的，宇宙大爆炸。

English Literary Translation · 英文译文

Where does our light come from? It suddenly occurred to me that the brightness and darkness outside us may have been given by someone from an age beyond ages. Then where does our own light arise? Let it begin with my practice now. What is the Great Perfection? What is the supreme wisdom of complete perfection? I had known these names for almost thirty years without understanding them, yet I had always loved them. Whenever I heard or saw them, I felt longing, though I did not know precisely what they meant. I hoped that by studying Buddhism, Daoism, Christianity, Hinduism and other traditions I might understand. Today, at last, I understood: in looking at this world, this universe, I had divided self from other. This question troubled me for decades; it was a pain carried from childhood onward, and I could not resolve it. Today I see it. Twenty years ago a friend said I would awaken to something; perhaps now I have. One day, seeking quiet, I closed the bathroom door and found myself in darkness. Again the thought came: perhaps the light and dark outside were given by someone from inconceivably long ago—but where does my own light come from? Let it begin with my practice now. I spoke of this sudden thought with several clients, and they too seemed touched by an insight. Today, through writing, I understood: perhaps it is not the Buddha who saves me; I save and carry myself across. Light exists of itself, is possessed from the beginning, and depends on nothing outside. Perhaps it is a recurrence of that first cosmic explosion.

中文原文 · Chinese Original

时光飞流而逝没有拖沓，如河，逝者如斯夫。把每一个懵懵懂懂的穿着红衣服小丫头与小小子，变成了系着红色围巾略显婀娜的姑娘与充满阳刚气的小伙子。韶光已逝，那可掬的笑容会依然如故的，那么佛法不就是让这时光不再飞逝，停留在我们希望停留的一点，一个年龄，那永远的十六岁，我们以光速行进在路上，即我们永远在修行，永远在发一颗菩提心，那不就是成就了吗？愿我们从过去中走出，追随太阳阳光的脚步，那会尽显我们的年轻。

English Literary Translation · 英文译文

Time streams away without delay, like a river—“passing like this.” Every bewildered little girl and boy in red becomes a graceful young woman or a vigorous young man wearing a red scarf. Youth passes, yet perhaps that radiant smile remains. Is the Dharma not a way for time to cease fleeing, to rest at the point and age where we wish to remain—forever sixteen? If we travel the road at the speed of light, always practicing and always awakening the mind of bodhi, is that not accomplishment? May we step out from the past and follow the footsteps of the sun, revealing the fullness of our youth.

三｜从前的光明在我们的心中 The Light of Former Times Is Within Us

中文原文 · Chinese Original

从前的光明在我们的心中。我不知道从何时起，喜爱之情就转到一直见到的阳光。因为寒冷地区的冬天吗？那个时候我与太阳🌞鸟的距离并没有远离，因为相对于地球🌍与太阳🌞，似乎这点远离不算什么。那是为什么呢？那红润火热的阳光，她在百年前也是这样照耀着这低矮的充满绿色的山上吧！那当然是啊。一排排的低矮的各式各样的二层小楼，带着颜色的，似乎应该是没有现在的带着后现代艺术创作风格的画。橱窗与陈列的设计应该是比较古典的吧！就像现在古董店里面卖的物什，带有圆形的曲线，不论是面，还是支撑的腿。喇叭📢声音还没有，喇喇叭声或许是那马车的铃铛🔔声音，因为1920年，那个时候汽车🚗还没有大规模生产使用。留声机传出来的应该是更早的古典或者声或许是那马车的铃铛🔔声音，因为1920年，那个时候汽车🚗还没有大规模生产使用。留声机传出来的应该是更早的古典或者。

English Literary Translation · 英文译文

The light of former times lives within us. I do not know when my affection turned toward the sunlight I saw each day. Was it because of winters in a cold region? My distance from the sun-bird had not truly grown; measured against the distance between Earth and Sun, such a separation was almost nothing. Why, then? That warm, ruddy sunlight must have shone a century ago upon these same low green hills. Of course it did. Rows of small, varied, two-storey houses would have stood in colour, without today’s postmodern designs. Their windows and displays were probably classical, like objects in an antique shop, with curved surfaces and rounded legs. There were not yet loudspeakers; perhaps the sound was the ringing of carriage bells. In 1920 automobiles were not yet in widespread use, and the gramophone would have carried older classical sounds into the street.

中文原文 · Chinese Original

想思的记忆。在这里，在这时，当我向着那开阔的聖劳伦河水望去的时候，蓝莲花似乎在远方的地平线出现。那是由山由水而构成的蓝莲花啊！那是一个我曾经从我有着萌萌蒙蒙如草芽如明亮月光出现前的蒙昧的时候，我就问自己的一个问题，我是这物？我是你吗？我的心爱之物的快美，是它自己的美乐，还是我头脑的快乐？大概按照机械唯物论的说法，是快乐通过我的神经的传导到我的脑中。可是我相问问答，我的脑中的快乐又传递到何方呢？还是按照现代生物学研究八爪鱼每个肢体都有其神经控制中心自己直接感受痛苦😣与快乐呢？那雨中的远方与我有缘的山水啊，我的心都的脉冲是否你与我同意，同样的意思与我？我们的低频信号的频率是否一致？1赫兹，5赫兹，还是以超音波的频率与你共振。

English Literary Translation · 英文译文

A memory of longing and thought. Here and now, as I look over the open waters of the St. Lawrence, a blue lotus seems to rise on the distant horizon—a blue lotus formed of mountain and river. Since the dim beginning of awareness, before I emerged like a grass shoot or moonlight, I have asked: Am I this thing? Am I you? Does the beauty and joy of what I love belong to the thing itself, or to the pleasure in my mind? A mechanical materialist might say that pleasure travels along my nerves into my brain. But then where does the pleasure in my brain go? Biology tells us that each arm of an octopus has its own neural centre and can directly feel pain and pleasure. Distant rain-soaked mountains and waters, bound to me by affinity—does the pulse of my heart agree with yours, mean what you mean? Are our low-frequency signals the same: one hertz, five hertz, or an ultrasonic frequency through which I resonate with you?

四｜山水、史前与时间 Landscape, Prehistory and Time

中文原文 · Chinese Original

那么一千年前，这里是什么？应该只是一片山岭云雨，因为丛林密布，应该是雨水丰沛。树林里面小河淌水，或急或缓，就像现在我们依旧在树林中见到的溪水潺潺一样。皇家山的朝南的一面原来是有一条小河的，一直流到聖劳伦河的，现在还有一个路的名字叫atwater，大概就是这条小河的纪念吧！虽然就是这么几座百米高低的平缓的小山，但是也孕育了起伏着浪花的流通的小河，小孩子的生辰是早晨，风水先生点明了，早晨八点是龙🐲时辰，对应着附近有一条小河。时空对应是不二的奇妙。一万年前呢？这里是冰河时期，漫山遍野的是厚重的冰雪覆盖，一片茫茫的景色。没有优美也没有迷人之处。太阳🌞光芒不足以融化这个冰雪世界的寒冷，或许任何动物也还没有出现在这里。那十万年前呢？十万年前这里的山应该比现在高吧！但是也不会高到哪里去，比如风化速度一万年一米，那么十万年前大概比现在高几十米吧！那个时候可是欧亚大陆尼安特人的时代，这里没有人的足迹🐾，史前，我们习惯说的，我们人类的有记载文字的历史之前，史前动物，现在还存在的美洲狮，还有或许已经灭绝的动物，欢乐的应该是没有忧伤的存在着，因为他们没有像我一样，思考😔在时空的里里外外，只是为果腹以及繁衍而生息着。那百万年前呢？中生代的时候呢？寒武纪的时候呢？寒武纪是生命大爆发的时代，几亿年前，那个时候我在哪里呢？记得三叶虫就是那个时候出现的，现在澳大利亚还有一种与三叶虫类似的动物在沙漠中存在着。我是三叶虫的一个成员吗？在仅仅的为找到海藻而高兴吗？不，没有高兴没有哀伤，仅仅的一种程序化的生活吗？不，我想我根本不是吧！不敢想象呀！

English Literary Translation · 英文译文

What was this place a thousand years ago? Probably only mountains, cloud and rain. Dense forest would have brought abundant water, and little rivers would have moved through the trees, sometimes swift and sometimes slow, like the streams we still hear today. A river once ran down the southern face of Mount Royal to the St. Lawrence. Perhaps the road named Atwater preserves its memory. These are only a few gentle hills, a hundred metres high, yet they gave birth to flowing rivers whose small waves rose and fell. A child was born in the morning; a feng-shui master noted that eight o’clock was the hour of the dragon, corresponding to a nearby river. The correspondence of time and space is a nondual wonder. Ten thousand years ago this was an ice age, the hills buried under heavy snow and ice, a boundless, austere landscape.
