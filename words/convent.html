① Literary Introduction / Author Observation（作者原始观察）

Original observation · 原始观察（不是已核词源）

convent，convention。习俗从修道院而来？

Retained as research provenance. No literary work is added here.

② Basic Meaning（基本词义）

Standard Translation: 修道院／女修道院

a religious community, especially of women, or its residence

Lexical identity: English convent.

③ Multilingual Mapping（多语言对应）

convent → 修道院／女修道院. Context determines the translation; this is not a historical cognacy claim.

④ Etymology（词源）

convent 与 convention 共享 convenire 来源，但 conventio 是独立分支；“习俗从修道院而来”被拒绝。会／聚只比较历史 ASSEMBLY，不能替代现代修道院义。
