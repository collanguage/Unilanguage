④ Etymology（词源）

Latin forma → Anglo-French furme/forme → Middle English forme → English form. Farm instead follows ferme/fermer ← Latin firmare/firmus; it is not a stage in this form lineage.

Historical / lexical branches
  1. Latin forma · Latin · Antiquity: form / shape / beauty.
  2. furme / forme · Anglo-French / Middle English · Medieval; English attested 13th century: shape / form.
  3. form · Modern English · Present day: shape / arrangement / mode; verb give shape.
