② Basic Meaning（基本词义）

Standard Translation: 商人／商贩

merchant, trader; commercial

Lexical identity: French marchand.

③ Multilingual Mapping（多语言对应）

marchand → 商人／商贩. Context determines the translation; this is not a historical cognacy claim.

④ Etymology（词源）

marchand 属商业来源；*mercatare / *mercatantem 是重建形式，星号必须保留。marcher 的行走来源独立；旅行商人的场景不是派生证明。贸对应 TRADE，商／贩对应 MERCHANT，不能混同动作与人物。

Historical stages / 完整历史基础
  1. mercatus — Latin; Classical Latin. MARKET / COMMERCE. Evidence: mediated; attested.
  2. *mercatare / *mercatantem — Reconstructed Vulgar Latin; Pre-Romance reconstruction; not a dated attestation. TO TRADE / TRADING PERSON. Evidence: mediated; reconstructed. Chinese Mapping: Not selected.
  3. marchand — French; Late 10th-century marchedant; modern spelling documented later → present. MERCHANT / DEALER (noun); trading (adjective separately). Evidence: mediated; attested.
