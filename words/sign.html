④ Etymology（词源）

Noun: Latin signum → Anglo-French signe → Middle English signe → sign. Verb: Latin signare, derived from signum, → Anglo-French signer → English sign. Cognition follows Latin cognitio; the observed ign/ogn strings do not establish a shared root here. Deeper signum etymology remains uncertain.

Historical / lexical branches
  1. signum / signare · Latin · Antiquity; noun and derived verb: mark, token / mark, sign.
  2. signe / signer · Anglo-French · Medieval: sign / mark.
  3. sign · English · 13th century onward; current senses separately scoped: mark / indication / conventional symbol; sign a name.

These are explicitly scoped branches and comparisons. Transformation arrows below are not historical chronology.
