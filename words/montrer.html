② Basic Meaning（基本词义）

Standard Translation: 显示／展示／指出（依语境）

to show, display or make known

Lexical identity: French montrer.

③ Multilingual Mapping（多语言对应）

montrer → 显示／展示／指出（依语境）. Context determines the translation; this is not a historical cognacy claim.

④ Etymology（词源）

montrer 延续 monstrare；monstrare 由 monstrum 构成，与 monere 有较深拉丁词族关系。monitor 经 Latin monitor / monere，是旁支而不是 montrer 的祖先或直接后代。SHOW、WARN、WATCH 不排成一条假的年代链。

Historical stages / 完整历史基础

  2. mostrer / monstrer — Old / Middle French; 10th-century show attestation; Académie monstrer spelling until 1718. SHOW / EXPLAIN / MAKE KNOWN. Evidence: mediated; attested. Chinese Mapping: Not selected.
  3. montrer — French; Académie spelling from 1740 → present. SHOW / POINT OUT / MAKE VISIBLE. Evidence: mediated; attested.
