Universe

宇宙 · cosmos · univers

Version 1.1 · Multidimensional Mapping Case · 多维映射案例 · 2026-08-16

English

The universe gathers space, time, matter, life and thought into one word. Each language divides—and reconnects—that totality in a different way.

中文

“宇宙”一词把空间、时间、物质、生命与思想汇集为一个整体。不同语言以不同方式切分这个整体，也以不同方式重新连接它。

Literary Introduction · 文学引言

The universe is a poem written by existence itself.
宇宙，是存在自己写成的一首诗。

02

Basic Meaning · 基本意义

Universe · 宇宙

all space, time, matter and energy considered as a whole

作为整体理解的全部空间、时间、物质与能量

Cosmos · 宇宙／秩序世界

the universe, often with an emphasis on order or an organized whole

宇宙；常带有秩序或有组织整体的意味

宇 yǔ

house, eaves, or spatial extent in historical and later usage

历史及后世用法中的屋宇、檐部或空间范围

宙 zhòu

an enduring temporal dimension in the compound 宇宙

在“宇宙”复合词中表示延续的时间维度

04

Etymology and Character Structure · 词源与字形结构

Latin-side history

English universe comes through French and Latin forms associated with Latin universum. The familiar analytic gloss “one + turned” is useful for explaining a whole brought into unity, but it should remain tied to documented Latin morphology rather than expanded into an unsupported universal sound theory.

Established · 已确立

拉丁语侧历史

英语 universe 经法语与拉丁语形式，可追溯至拉丁语 universum。“一＋转”的常用分析有助于解释“合为一个整体”，但应停留在有文献支持的拉丁语形态范围内，不应扩展为无证据的普遍声音理论。

Established · 已确立

Chinese character structure

宇 and 宙 both contain 宀. In conventional character analysis, 由 functions as the phonetic component of 宙. This supports a script-level relationship between 宙 and 由; it does not by itself prove that all meanings of 由 belong to 宙.

Supported · 有支持

汉字结构

“宇”和“宙”都含有“宀”。在常规字形分析中，“由”在“宙”中充当声符。这支持“宙—由”的文字结构关系；但不能仅凭声符就把“由”的全部意义归入“宙”。

Supported · 有支持

Evidence boundary · 证据边界： Script structure, lexical history and later philosophical interpretation are separate evidence layers. · 字形结构、词汇历史与后来的哲学解释属于不同证据层。

01 · Discovery · 发现

Historical, script, sound and cultural layers · 历史、文字、语音与文化层

Historical Etymology · 历史词源Latin unus + versus → one + turned → whole

English universe continues through Old French and Latin universum. Here versus is related to turning; the element verse is not reinterpreted as “voice.” · 英语 universe 经古法语承接拉丁语 universum；此处 versus 与“转向”相关，不把 verse 直接解释成“声音”。

Established · 已确立
Chinese semantic structure · 汉语语义结构宇 + 宙 → SPACE + TIME → TOTALITY

The compound is interpreted as a totality structured by spatial extension and temporal duration. · “宇宙”被解释为空间延展与时间延续共同构成的整体。

Supported · 有支持
Sound Before Writing · 声音先于文字Speech predates writing; a specific sound–meaning history still requires evidence.

Framework principle: Homophony is evidence to investigate, not evidence to conclude. · 框架原则：“同音是值得调查的证据，不是可以直接下结论的证据。” See Sound Protocol · 声音协议.

Established premise + Speculative extension · 已确立前提＋推测性延伸
Script relation · 文字关系宙 ← 由 (phonetic component · 声符)

A supported character-structure relation, not unrestricted semantic identity. · 有支持的字形结构关系，并非不受限制的语义同一。

Supported · 有支持
Homophonic-semantic mapping · 同音语义映射宙 zhòu ↔ 咒 zhòu

Modern Mandarin homophony permits an experimental association between universe, utterance and incantation; it does not establish shared etymology. · 现代普通话同音允许在宇宙、言说与咒语之间进行实验性联想，但不证明共同词源。

Experimental · 实验性
Collanguage / Unilanguage hypothesis · Collanguage／Unilanguage 假说由 / 游 / 悠 / 油 → FLOW / CONTINUITY candidate network

This network compares sound resemblance and semantic tendencies in modern forms. It is a testable exploratory proposal, not historical etymology or proof of a shared origin. · 这一网络比较现代形式的语音近似与语义倾向；它是可检验的探索性提案，不是历史词源，也不证明共同来源。

Experimental · 实验性
Cultural / Religious Interpretation · 文化／宗教解释Word · Logos · mantra · creation speech

These traditions provide documented cultural models in which word, sound or utterance participates in order or creation. Treating them as one historical system would be speculative. · 这些传统提供了“词、声音或言说参与秩序或创造”的文化模型；把它们视为同一个历史系统则属推测。

02 · Creation · 创造

Literary proposition and new semantic relation · 文学命题与新语义关系

Created proposition

The universe is a poem written by existence itself.

The sentence deliberately maps existence to an author, the universe to a poem, and cosmic structure to language.

被创造的命题

宇宙，是存在自己写成的一首诗。

这一句主动把“存在”映射为作者，把“宇宙”映射为诗，把宇宙结构映射为语言。

Creation boundary · 创造边界： This is a reusable literary and philosophical creation, not a claim about the historical origin of universe or 宇宙. · 这是可复用的文学与哲学创造，不是关于 universe 或“宇宙”历史起源的判断。

Translation Guidance · 翻译指导

English → Chinese

universe normally translates as 宇宙. Cosmos may also be 宇宙, while contexts emphasizing order may require explanatory wording.

中文 → 英文

“宇宙”通常译为 universe；若语境强调秩序化整体，可根据上下文选择 cosmos。

UTP note · UTP 说明： 宙—咒 and Logos/mantra mappings are interpretive tools, not default lexical translations. A dedicated Universe UTP case is planned. · “宙—咒”及 Logos／mantra 映射属于解释工具，不是默认词义翻译；Universe 专项 UTP 案例待建。
