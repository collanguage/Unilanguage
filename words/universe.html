Research archive · Historical Etymology · 历史词源

Documented Latin structure

Universus means “all together, whole, entire” and is analyzed as unus plus versus, the participial form of vertere: “turned into one; combined into one whole.” English universe continues Latin universum; French univers belongs to the same Latin history. [1] [2] [3]

Established · High confidence

可靠边界

可发表的历史链是：universus → unus + versus ← vertere → ONE + TURNED → WHOLE。这里的 vers-/vert- 历史核心是 TURN／TURNED；它不能自动推出“宇宙在旋转”的物理结论。

历史词源已确立

What about modern verse? · 现代 verse 呢？

English verse “a poetic line” belongs to the wider historical family through Latin versus “line/row,” itself related to turning. That later poetic sense is not the operative modern meaning in the decomposition of universe. [4]
