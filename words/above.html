④ Etymology（词源）

Old English abufan (a- + bufan; bufan from be- + ufan) → Middle English above/aboven → above. Over is a related Germanic comparison, not an intervening ancestor.

Historical / lexical branches
  1. abufan · Old English · Before 12th century: above / higher location.
  2. above / aboven · Middle English · Medieval: higher location; relational use.
  3. above · Modern English · Present day: higher than in location, rank or quantity.

These are explicitly scoped branches and comparisons. Transformation arrows below are not historical chronology.
