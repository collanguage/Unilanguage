Methodology notes

Independent passes, frozen labels, blinded comparison

01

Isolated annotation

AI-A and AI-B used separate contexts and distinct pre-existing randomized orders. Neither pass received group assignment, pilot outcomes, the restricted analysis key, or the other pass's output.

02

Frozen coding scheme

Both passes used only HUMAN, PERSON, PEOPLE, IDENTITY, HUMAN-ATTRIBUTE, and UNCERTAIN under the frozen definitions.

03

Freeze before comparison

Both 1,020-row outputs were structurally validated and hashed before inter-pass comparison. Agreement was joined by blind identifier, not row position.
