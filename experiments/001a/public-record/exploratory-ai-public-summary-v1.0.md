# Experiment 001A Round 2 — Public-Safe Summary｜公开安全摘要

## English

**Exploratory AI Result — Tentatively Supported**

**Preregistered Human Annotation: Pending**

The first authorized exploratory AI unblinding found the same modest positive direction in both frozen AI passes:

- **AI-A:** M 76/510 (14.90%) vs Controls 60/510 (11.76%); risk difference **+3.14 percentage points**, 95% CI **−1.05 to +7.33**; one-sided Fisher **p=0.0835**.
- **AI-B:** M 79/510 (15.49%) vs Controls 61/510 (11.96%); risk difference **+3.53 percentage points**, 95% CI **−0.71 to +7.77**; one-sided Fisher **p=0.0608**.

The prespecified evidence-family robustness analysis retained nearly the same positive differences: **+3.42 pp** for AI-A and **+3.47 pp** for AI-B. Both 95% intervals still included zero.

This is a directionally consistent but uncertain exploratory signal. It does not confirm or reject the preregistered human hypothesis. AI-A and AI-B were analyzed separately, and neither pass was selected, merged, or adjudicated after unblinding.

The raw restricted analysis key, row-level identities, joined data, and row-level AI annotations remain private to preserve future human blinding. Experiment 001B was not started.
