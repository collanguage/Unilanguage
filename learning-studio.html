Our Method · 我们的方法

Unilanguage combines language learning with research and creative exploration. Original hypotheses, AI-generated proposals and learner ideas must remain distinct from independently verified evidence.

Unilanguage 将语言学习与研究、创造相结合。原创假说、AI 生成提案和学习者创意，必须与独立核验的证据明确区分。
