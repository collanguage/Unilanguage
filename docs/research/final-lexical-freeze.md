## Freeze / stop decision

- Featured: **Pending for all four**, independently of Reviewed entry and Published availability.
- Reader display: Standard + Featured Pending + two meaningful stage comparisons. Other candidates and controls remain in Research/Evidence.
- Old observations are preserved verbatim in `source.raw_note` and the reversible `legacy_migration` archive. The misleading combined semantic chains are replaced with explicit branching boundaries.

- FIL: retain fil → filière; reject fille as a thread-family continuation.
- MARCHAND: downgrade walking/travelling association to Research/cognitive scene; no marcher ancestry.
- MONTRER: retain deeper Latin family relation, reject direct montrer → monitor derivation.
- No further candidate search merely to create Featured mappings. Large print dictionaries, earliest Chinese attestations and historical IPA remain Pending.

Consonant features were recorded only after semantic retrieval: modern k/h generation interest in CONVENT, f control for FIL, m/f interest for MARCHAND, m control for MONTRER. The existing group inventory is not redefined. A group feature on a modern form does not score an untranscribed Latin stage. This bounded sample does **not** establish improved precision or recall; four deliberately selected sound controls are semantic mismatches, not an estimated false-positive rate for an entire language. No group-derived candidate earns Featured status.
