## FORM

Standard: 形状 / 形式 / 结构；form a shape = 形成 / 塑造（依义项）

Latin forma → Anglo-French furme/forme → Middle English forme → English form. Farm instead follows ferme/fermer ← Latin firmare/firmus; it is not a stage in this form lineage.

形 xíng = shape; 型 xíng = model/mould; 式 shì = pattern/format; 体 tǐ = body or mode in a specific construction. No single character covers all senses.

Abstract → Concrete → Shape → Structure is an illustrative design operation, NOT a historical chronology or a universal semantic law.

**Comparisons**

- 形 xíng: shape / visible form; semantic fit High; phonetic Low.
- 型 xíng: model / mould / type; semantic fit High within model sense; phonetic Low.
- 式 shì: pattern / prescribed format; semantic fit High within pattern sense; phonetic Low.
- 体 tǐ: body / mode / organized whole; semantic fit Limited; construction-dependent; phonetic Low.

**Counterinterpretation**

- A tax form is a 表格, not simply 形.
- A form of government concerns organization; a human form concerns shape.
- farm/form resemblance does not override their separately documented immediate sources.

**Rejected / retained as Raw**

- farm as historical ancestor of form; preserved as Raw Research observation

## SIGN

Standard: 标记 / 符号 / 迹象；sign a name = 签名（依义项）

Noun: Latin signum → Anglo-French signe → Middle English signe → sign. Verb: Latin signare, derived from signum, → Anglo-French signer → English sign. Cognition follows Latin cognitio; the observed ign/ogn strings do not establish a shared root here. Deeper signum etymology remains uncertain.

标 biāo / 记 jì: mark; 符 fú: conventional symbol; 征 zhēng (徵): indication. 象 xiàng is a representation comparison, not a universal translation.

Concept → Mark → Symbol → Meaning models a representation/interpretation task. It is NOT the historical order of sign senses.

**Comparisons**

- 记 jì: mark / notation in 记号; not every remembering sense; semantic fit Limited; phonetic Low.
- 标 biāo: mark / identifying marker; semantic fit High within mark sense; phonetic Low.
- 符 fú: conventional symbol, as in 符号; semantic fit High within symbol sense; phonetic Low.
- 象 xiàng: representation / image; semantic fit Limited; not every sign is an image; phonetic Low.
- 征 zhēng: indication, as in 征兆/特征 (traditional 徵); semantic fit High within indication sense; phonetic Low.

**Counterinterpretation**

- Smoke may be a sign of fire without being a designed symbol.
- Sign your name requires 签名, not a blanket substitution with 象 or 符.
- cognition/ign/ogn spelling is not evidence that cognoscere is an ancestor of sign.

**Rejected / retained as Raw**

- ign/ogn as an established shared sign/cognition root
- 象 as a whole-word equivalent for every sense

**Sources**

- [SIGN](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/sign)
- [COGNITION](https://www.cnrtl.fr/definition/cognitions)

## PRESS

Standard: 按 / 压；催促 / 逼迫（依语境）；本条聚焦施力动词

Latin premere / its frequentative pressare → Anglo-French presser → English press. French pression is a related nominal branch, not an obligatory ancestor. The enlistment homograph (press into service) has a separate prest history and is outside this selected lexical identity.

按 àn / 压 yā compare hand contact and applied pressure. Force, pressure and compression are related but are not interchangeable physical quantities or outcomes.

压 yā / 逼 bī / 催 cuī compare constraint and urging by sense. Physical Force → Constraint / Urgency is an interpretive transformation, NOT verified chronology or necessary causation.

**Comparisons**

- 压 yā: apply pressure / constrain; not yà in 压根儿; semantic fit High within pressure sense; phonetic Low.
- 按 àn: press with the hand / hold down; semantic fit High within contact sense; phonetic Low.
- 逼 bī: compel / constrain; semantic fit High within coercion sense; phonetic Low.
- 催 cuī: urge / hurry; semantic fit High within urgency sense; phonetic Low.

**Counterinterpretation**

- CNRTL records an abstract presser attestation around 1160 and a fruit-compressing use around 1200; those attestations do not support a simple physical-first French chronology.
- Press the button does not entail fear or urgency.
- peur/怕 concerns fear, not the lexical meaning or ancestry of press.

**Rejected / retained as Raw**

- peur / 怕 as the historical basis of press
- physical → abstract as a demonstrated strict chronology

## ABOVE

Standard: 在……上方 / 高于 / 超过（依关系与语境）

Old English abufan (a- + bufan; bufan from be- + ufan) → Middle English above/aboven → above. Over is a related Germanic comparison, not an intervening ancestor.

ABOVE(x,y) compares x with a reference y: 在……之上 / 高于. 上 shàng is a sense-specific comparison; Mandarin construction matters.

Interface to SPACE / UP / DOWN / RELATION: ABOVE is a relative-position relation; UP may indicate direction. Protocol relation ≠ historical etymology. OVER is not interchangeable in every construction.

**Comparisons**

- 上 shàng: higher location; not shǎng tone category; semantic fit High within spatial sense; phonetic Low.
- 在……之上 zài…zhī shàng: relative higher location; semantic fit High within relation; phonetic Low.
- 高于 gāoyú: higher than; selected scalar comparisons; semantic fit High within comparison; phonetic Low.

**Counterinterpretation**

- A lamp above a table need not touch it; a book on the table may do so.
- above suspicion is not a literal higher coordinate.
- UP direction and ABOVE relative position are not the same grammatical operation.

**Rejected / retained as Raw**

- above → over → 上 as a historical ancestry chain
- forced phonetic winner

**Sources**

- [ABOVE](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/above)

## LIGHT

Standard: 光 / 光亮（brightness noun；不含“重量轻”同形词）

English light (brightness) ← Middle English light ← Old English lēoht/līht, inherited Germanic; deeper reconstructed *leuk- family. French lumière ← Christian Latin luminaria (plural of luminare), related to lumen. Latin lux is a related family member, NOT an intermediate ancestor of lumière. Chinese 籁 has its own instrument/sound senses.

English light → 光 guāng is the brightness sense. French lumière is a separate Romance form; lux is related, not a link inserted into its derivation.

light /laɪt/ ↔ 籁 lài: limited segmental resemblance, English final /t/ and Mandarin tone differ; brightness and sound differ. lumière /ly.mjɛʁ/ ↔ 籁 is weaker. L→光 remains Untested / Pending; poetry is motivation, not a result.

《如光天籁》 by Jinkai Liu remains Author’s Literary / Semantic Creation. LIGHT ↔ SOUND / MUSIC is free artistic interpretation, not historical or experimental evidence.

**Comparisons**

- 光 guāng: light / brightness; semantic fit High: standard semantic mapping; phonetic Low.
- 籁 lài: instrument / sound, as in 天籁 / 万籁; semantic fit Low lexical; interpretive cross-modal bridge; phonetic Low.

Exception: light↔籁 has limited segmental resemblance, but low lexical fit; lumière↔籁 remains low phonetic fit. Neither is Featured.

**Counterinterpretation**

- 籁 denotes instrument/sound, not the normal Chinese word for visible light.
- light has final /t/; lài has lexical tone. lumière has a different syllable structure and vowels.
- English light and French/Latin light words are not independent positive trials once family membership is controlled.

**Rejected / retained as Raw**

- lux as an intermediate ancestor of lumière
- poetry as historical or experimental proof
- lumière ↔ 籁 as a strong phonetic match
