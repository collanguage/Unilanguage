## Template

1. **Standard Meaning** first: lexical identity, selected sense and normal translation. For polyphonic Chinese, print the reading. Standard semantic support does not establish strong phonetic fit.
2. **Featured / Primary Mapping**: an editorial summary of a scoped comparison, never a synonym for standard translation or strongest evidence. Show its actual type. It may belong to a lower exploratory layer; preserve the canonical selection without placing it above the standard meaning.
3. **Diachronic Stage Mappings**: identify source form, language and relevant sense. State why a Chinese form appears and what it does not translate. Coexisting senses are not automatically a timeline.
4. **Additional layers**: render only recorded Structural, Cultural, Dialect or Literary material. Attribute author examples. Keep observation, independent verification, location and Pending separate. Do not invent absent layers.
5. **Evidence Boundary**: attach Supported/Candidate/Author-attested/Pending to their actual claims. Reuse existing status-chip/status styles; do not use color as the sole status signal. Historical Relation is a separate axis.
6. **Research Status**: Published entry does not mean Proven mapping. Link detailed references, counterexamples, scores and source records below the readable summary.

All cross-language historical relations remain Not claimed. Original Featured 火 remains Candidate/D/Low in the data and is explicitly identified within the author-observation layer. ## CONVENIR compatibility preview — not a page or data edit

Based solely on the approved CONVENIR calibration, not a new linguistic search:

- **Modern convenir:** suitability, agreement and acknowledgement are distinct constructions/senses.
- **Featured summary:** 合 hé — limited FIT / BE SUITABLE; Candidate, phonetic Low, Historical Relation Not claimed.
- **Historical semantic units:** Latin gathering and fitting/agreement may coexist; Old French suitability and later reported acknowledgement have their own documentation. Do not display COME TOGETHER→AGREE→SUIT as a proven timeline.
- **Stage comparisons:** gathering ↔ 聚／会／合; fitting ↔ 合／宜; agreement and acknowledgement kept separate. No requirement that 合 cover acknowledgement.
- **Chinese diachrony:** show the existing textual/graph/edition gaps as Pending. Do not turn modern 合适 into an ancient attestation.
- **Evidence boundary:** semantic/structural parallels do not establish common origin. Featured is a summary of the stage record.

Compatibility passes conceptually: ABHOR's author/dialect block is optional; CONVENIR instead needs multiple stage rows with source/Chinese layer labels and visible unmapped/Pending rows. The template already permits this. No CONVENIR page created, no accepted data or depth classification changed.
