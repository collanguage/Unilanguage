## Evidence conclusion

The cited bilingual dictionaries support context-bound lexical comparisons only. They do not support shared origin, borrowing, regular phonetic correspondence, cognitive universals, or speculative Unilanguage associations. Phonetic evidence is therefore `not_assessed` for every record, speculative association is `not_supported`, and project protocol text is treated as internal/partial semantic-cognitive support rather than independent empirical evidence.
