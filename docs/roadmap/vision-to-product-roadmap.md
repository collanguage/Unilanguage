# Unilanguage Vision-to-Product Roadmap | 共通语言愿景与产品路线
Author / 原始构想: Jinkai Liu
Status / 状态: Proposed roadmap; not a claim of proven scientific results
Date: 2026-10-24

## Mission | 使命
1. Discover shared semantic structures among human languages. / 发现人类语言之间的共通语义结构。
2. Build a language-independent semantic protocol. / 建立不依赖单一自然语言的语义协议。
3. Explore a future common symbolic language through learning and human collaboration, potentially relevant to spacefaring communities. / 通过学习与人类协作探索未来共通符号语言，并研究其在太空社会的潜在用途。

## Phase A — Discovery foundation | 发现基础
Keep Language Book, Protocol Book, Semantic Mapper, Hypothesis Lab and UTP Lab as separate but linked systems. Clearly distinguish entry publication, mapping status, historical evidence and literary expression. Do not alter frozen experiment packages.
保持语言书、协议书、映射器、假说实验室及翻译实验室的独立性与关联性。严格区分词条发表、映射状态、历史证据与文学表达。不修改已冻结实验数据。

## Phase B — Learning MVP | 学习产品
Prototype bilingual/trilingual learning journeys built from reviewed entries; test vocabulary comprehension and retention, and label speculative mnemonic associations. No paid product is claimed to exist yet.
以已审核词条制作英汉法学习原型，检验词义理解与记忆效果；将实验性联想明确标注。当前不宣称已有收费产品。

## Phase C — Community co-creation | 社区共创
Design a contribution flow: submit idea → tag mapping type → supply sources → search counterexamples → editorial review → candidate/reviewed/published. Include consent, attribution, moderation, privacy and anti-spam before public submissions.
设计贡献流程：提出想法→标注映射类型→提交来源→寻找反例→编辑审核→候选/已审核/发表。开放投稿前先完善授权、署名、审核、隐私与反垃圾机制。

## Phase D — Semantic protocol and symbols | 语义协议与符号
Evaluate semantic primitives and multimodal representations. Keep designed symbols distinct from claims of natural-language universals.
评估基础语义单元与多模态表达；将人工设计符号与自然语言普遍规律的科学主张区分。

## Phase E — Space-oriented scenarios | 航天场景
Study realistic multilingual, human-machine and safety-critical communication requirements in future space habitats; validate against actual users and standards before claiming suitability.
研究未来太空居住场景中的多语言、人机和安全关键交流需求；未经实际用户及标准验证，不宣称已适用。


## Language Heritage | 语言遗产（规划专区）
Purpose: Document and teach living linguistic diversity alongside shared semantic patterns; avoid presenting any living language as a static “fossil”. / 目标：在探索共通语义结构的同时记录与教授活态语言多样性，不把任何活语言视为静止的“化石”。

Initial collections / 初步专题：
- Classical expressions and historical meanings / 典籍词汇与历史语义
- Regional varieties and dialect expressions (with speaker, locality and usage context) / 地方方言与地域表达（记录使用者、地区与语境）
- Miao, Yi and other languages through collaboration with speakers / 与苗语、彝语等语言使用者合作
- Eurasian linguistic and cultural exchanges / 欧亚语言与文化交流

Suggested record fields / 建议字段：language/variety, local name, region, original writing or transcription, IPA/audio (with consent), gloss, example, cultural context, attestation/source, contributor attribution, evidence status, editorial status, rights and consent. / 语言及变体、本地名称、地区、原文或转写、国际音标或经授权的音频、释义、例句、文化语境、文献来源、贡献者署名、证据状态、编辑状态、权利与同意。

Editorial safeguards / 编辑边界：Do not assume shared etymology from similar meaning. Record uncertainty and competing interpretations. Respect language communities and protect sensitive cultural material. No public submissions or recordings before consent and moderation mechanisms are ready. / 不因意义相近便推断同源；记录不确定性与不同解释；尊重语言社群及敏感文化材料；未建立授权和审核机制前不开放投稿或录音收集。

Implementation order / 实施顺序：1. Static bilingual landing page and editorial schema; 2. Curated sample entries with verified sources; 3. Community-reviewed contributions; 4. Learning activities linked to Language Book. / 先建立双语入口与编辑结构，再做有来源的示范词条，随后逐步开放审核投稿与语言学习功能。

## Release boundaries | 发布边界
This roadmap and vision may be reviewed independently. Do not merge to main or publish to Netlify without separate authorization. Do not change research classifications, experiment freezes or corpus counts.
本路线图和愿景可以独立审核；未经单独授权，不合并主分支或发布至 Netlify；不改变研究分类、实验冻结状态和语料数量。
