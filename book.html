    Book Structure · 语言书结构

    1. Word Entry · 1. 词条

    Each word contains English, Chinese, French, pronunciation, category and semantic meaning. · 词条记录英、汉、法语形式、读音、类别及语义。

    2. Etymology · 2. 语源

    Trace the origin of words through English, French, Latin, Chinese and other language layers. · 从英语、法语、拉丁语、汉语等语言层面追溯词源。

    3. Semantic Mapping · 3. 语义映射

    Connect words through sound, image, direction, culture and meaning fields. · 通过声音、意象、方向、文化和语义领域联系词语。

    4. Protocol Rules · 4. 协议规则

    Explain how meaning is encoded, transmitted and reconstructed across systems. · 说明意义如何在不同系统之间编码、传递和重构。

    5. Literary Explanation · 5. 文学讲解

    Each word may begin with a poetic sentence, then unfold into linguistic analysis. · 词条可从一句诗意的文字展开，再进入语言分析。

    6. Media Layer · 6. 媒体层

    Future entries may include images, music, video explanation and AI narration. · 日后的词条可能包含图片、音乐、视频讲解和 AI 旁白。
