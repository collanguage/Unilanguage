    Learn with Unilanguage · 学习导览

    • Semantic Mapper · 语义映射器

      Type a recorded form or choose one from the English, Chinese and French indexes. Check whether the result is a Language Book record or an unpublished production candidate. The translation, proposed mapping, historical relationship and experiment each have their own status.

      输入已收录的词形，或从英、汉、法索引中选择一项。先核对结果是语言书词条，还是尚未出版的候选记录。通用译义、拟议的映射、历史关系和实验各有独立状态。

    • Protocol Book · 协议书

      Read how Unilanguage describes sound, space and semantic mappings. Start with Space for the SKY case, then use the mapping framework to see how the project records a mapping and its support. The theory chapters describe the project’s framework. Check the linked evidence before applying any historical claim.

      了解 Unilanguage 如何描述声音、空间和语义映射。可从「空间」一章中的 SKY 案例开始，再读映射框架，了解项目如何记录映射及其依据。理论章节介绍项目采用的框架；引用历史主张前，请核查对应证据。

    • UTP Lab · 翻译协议实验室

      Read the SKY evaluation to see how a proposed translation handles meaning and context. Compare the evaluation criteria with the linked Language Book entry and the Space chapter. A translation evaluation supports conclusions about the cases it examines.

      阅读 SKY 翻译评测，了解译法如何处理词义和语境。可把评测标准与对应语言书词条和「空间」章节对照阅读。翻译评测所得的结论适用于实际评测的案例。

    • Hypothesis Lab · 假说实验室

      Choose a registered hypothesis, read its prediction and evidence track, then open the linked design or result. A preregistration documents a planned test. An inconclusive result leaves the tested claim unresolved.

      选择一条已登记的假说，先阅读预测及其证据类别，再打开所链接的研究设计或结果。预注册记录计划实施的检验。若实验结果不确定，该主张仍未得到确证。

    • Knowledge Graph · 知识图谱

      Follow links among recorded concepts and project pages. Use the relevant Language Book entry and evidence references to check what a connection represents.

      沿图谱浏览已收录概念及相关项目页面。请查阅对应的语言书词条和证据引文，核对每条连接所代表的关系。

    • Evidence and references · 证据与参考资料

      Use this guide to check a source’s provenance and the exact claim it supports. Keep documented lexical history, phonetic and semantic observations, and speculative claims distinct when citing a record.

      用本页查看如何核对资料出处，以及资料具体支持哪项主张。引用记录时，请分别说明有文献依据的词汇史、语音与语义观察，以及推测性主张。

    The notes above summarize the linked pages. Some research and archival passages are available in their original languages.

    上面的导览概述了各页面的内容。部分研究和存档段落仅提供原文。

    Book Structure · 语言书结构

    1. Word Entry · 词条

    Each word contains English, Chinese, French, pronunciation, category and semantic meaning.

    2. Etymology · 语源

    Trace the origin of words through English, French, Latin, Chinese and other language layers.

    3. Semantic Mapping · 语义映射

    Connect words through sound, image, direction, culture and meaning fields.

    4. Protocol Rules · 协议规则

    Explain how meaning is encoded, transmitted and reconstructed across systems.

    5. Literary Explanation · 文学讲解

    Each word may begin with a poetic sentence, then unfold into linguistic analysis.

    6. Media Layer · 媒体层

    Future entries may include images, music, video explanation and AI narration.
