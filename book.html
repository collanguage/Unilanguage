    Book Structure · 语言书结构

    1. Word Entry · 词条

    Each word contains English, Chinese, French, pronunciation, category and semantic meaning.

    2. Etymology · 语源

    Trace the origin of words through English, French, Latin, Chinese and other language layers.

    3. Semantic Mapping · 语义映射

    Connect words through sound, image, direction, culture and meaning fields.

    4. Protocol Rules · 协议规则

    Explain how meaning is encoded, transmitted and reconstructed across systems.

    5. Literary Explanation · 文学讲解

    Each word may begin with a poetic sentence, then unfold into linguistic analysis.

    6. Media Layer · 媒体层

    Future entries may include images, music, video explanation and AI narration.
