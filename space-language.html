Space Language Initiative · 航天共通语言计划

A research proposal to explore how people interpret visual directions and spatial relations across languages.

这项研究提案旨在了解使用不同语言的人如何理解图形所表达的方向与空间关系。

Aspirational V1 target December 7, 2026. The date is a planning goal.

第一版力争于 2026 年 12 月 7 日 达到目标。该日期仅供规划参考。

Community Research · 共同研究与讨论

The exercise needs no account or submission. Lab choices stay in the current page and the Lab provides no upload or tracking.

参加练习无需注册或提交资料。你在实验室中的选择只保留在当前页面。实验室没有上传或追踪功能。

Anyone can try the exercise offline. When discussing it publicly, share only material you have permission to share.

任何人都可以离线尝试。参与公开讨论时，请只分享你有权公开的材料。

Your language preference is saved in this browser. Mapper links include the lookup text in the URL. Avoid entering sensitive text or sharing links that contain it.

语言偏好保存在当前浏览器。语义映射器的链接会在网址中包含查询文字，请避免输入敏感内容或分享含有敏感内容的链接。

  1. Sketch a candidate offline. Write down its intended meaning and one plausible alternative.
    离线绘制候选符号，记下预期含义和另一种可能的理解。
  2. With consent, show the sketch to a volunteer without the intended reading. Ask what they see. Participation is optional and they can stop at any time.
    先征得志愿者的同意，再展示草图并请对方说出自己的理解。此时先不透露你预期的含义。参与完全自愿，志愿者可以随时退出。
  3. Keep notes offline. If you choose to share publicly, share your own sketch, a public source link if relevant, and a summary with no names, contact details, identifying quotes, recordings or private materials.
    请将笔记留在本地。如自愿公开分享，只分享自己的草图、相关公开来源的链接和摘要。摘要中请省略姓名、联系方式、可能暴露身份的引语、录音和私人资料。
  4. Label your explanation as a hypothesis and record unexpected interpretations. An informal observation can guide a later test.
    将你的解释标注为假说，并记下出乎意料的解读。非正式观察可为后续检验提供线索。
