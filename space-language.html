Community Research · 社区研究

The exercise needs no account or submission. Lab choices stay in the current page and the Lab provides no upload or tracking.

练习无需账户或提交。实验室的选择仅保留在当前页面，页面没有上传或跟踪功能。

  1. Sketch a candidate offline. Write down its intended meaning and one plausible alternative.
    离线绘制候选符号，记下预期含义和另一种可能的理解。
  2. With consent, show the sketch to a volunteer without the intended reading. Ask what they see. Participation is optional and they can stop at any time.
    经同意后向志愿者展示草图，先不透露预期读法，再询问其理解。可自愿参与并随时退出。
  3. Keep notes offline. If you choose to share publicly, share your own sketch, a public source link if relevant, and a summary with no names, contact details, identifying quotes, recordings or private materials.
    笔记请留在本地。如选择公开分享，仅分享自己的草图、相关公开来源链接和摘要。请省略姓名、联系方式、可识别身份的原话、录音和私人材料。
  4. Label your explanation as a hypothesis and record unexpected interpretations. An informal observation can guide a later test.
    把解释标为假说，并记录意外的解读。非正式观察可以为后续测试提供线索。
