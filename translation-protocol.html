01

Purpose · 目的

English

The Unilanguage Translation Protocol is designed to evaluate and guide human and AI translation between English, Chinese, French and future languages.

It records not only whether a sentence is grammatically acceptable, but also which meanings, relations, metaphors and cultural signals survive the translation.

中文

共通语言翻译协议用于评估和指导人类与 AI 在英语、汉语、法语以及未来更多语言之间的翻译。

它不仅记录译文是否符合语法，也检验哪些意义、关系、隐喻和文化信号在翻译后得到保留。

02

Core Idea · 核心思想

English

Translation is not merely the replacement of one word with another. Translation reconstructs meaning across language, culture, sound, space, context and human cognition.

中文

翻译并不是简单地用一个词替换另一个词。翻译需要在语言、文化、声音、空间、语境与人类认知之间重构意义。

Translation = Encoding + Mapping + Reconstruction
翻译＝编码＋映射＋重构

03

Translation Layers · 翻译层级

L1

Literal Meaning · 字面意义

Preserve the direct lexical and grammatical content.

保留直接的词汇内容与语法信息。

L2

Semantic Meaning · 语义意义

Preserve the intended concept, relation and contextual sense.

保留意图中的概念、关系与语境义。

L3

Cultural Meaning · 文化意义

Identify cultural references, symbolism and differences in worldview.

识别文化典故、象征意义与世界观差异。

L4

Sound Mapping · 语音映射

Record relevant sound relations without treating resemblance as proof of cognacy.

记录相关语音关系，但不把语音相似当作同源证明。

L5

Protocol Mapping · 协议映射

Analyze semantic operations such as ABOVE, MOVE, COVER, BOUNDARY and CHANGE.

分析 ABOVE、MOVE、COVER、BOUNDARY、CHANGE 等语义关系和操作。

L6

Meaning Reconstruction · 意义重构

Select a translation strategy that preserves the most relevant layers for the context.

根据语境选择能够保留最重要意义层的翻译策略。

Evaluation Standard · 评测标准

20

Literal Accuracy · 字面准确度

Lexicon, grammar and explicit information.

词汇、语法与显性信息。

20

Semantic Preservation · 语义保留度

Concept, relation, intention and contextual meaning.

概念、关系、意图与语境意义。

20

Cultural Adequacy · 文化适切度

Cultural references, metaphor and register.

文化典故、隐喻与语体。

20

Structural Mapping · 结构映射度

Spatial, temporal, causal and relational structure.

空间、时间、因果与关系结构。

20

Protocol Consistency · 协议一致性

Clear reasoning, evidence labels and reproducible decisions.

清晰理由、证据标签与可重复判断。

100

Total Score · 总分

The total score must be accompanied by evidence and review status.

总分必须同时附带证据状态与审核状态。

Experimental · 实验性

Speculative · 推测性

Preliminary intuition · 初步直觉

目前主要来自联想或推测，不能写成事实结论。

Poetic / Cultural · 诗性／文化

Interpretive value · 具有解释与文化价值

保留文学、宗教、哲学和文化意义，但不作为历史同源证据。

Review Status · 审核状态

AI / Human / Expert · AI／人工／专家

说明判断是否经过 AI 初检、作者核查、人工复核或专家评审。

06

AI Translation Benchmark · AI 翻译基准

English

UTP compares AI systems with the same source text, context, scoring dimensions and evidence requirements.

  • Does the AI preserve literal accuracy?
  • Does it reconstruct contextual meaning?
  • Does it recognize metaphor and cultural signals?
  • Does it explain important structural choices?
  • Does it distinguish fact from hypothesis?
中文

UTP 使用相同原文、相同语境、相同评分维度和相同证据要求比较不同 AI 系统。

  • AI 是否保留字面准确性？
  • 是否重构了语境意义？
  • 是否识别隐喻与文化信号？
  • 是否解释重要的结构选择？
  • 是否区分事实与假说？
