01

Purpose · 目的

English

Translation is not only word replacement. UTP evaluates referential meaning, semantic structure, cultural meaning, sound mapping, protocol mapping and meaning reconstruction.

中文

翻译不只是词语替换。UTP 评估指称意义、语义结构、文化意义、声音映射、协议映射与意义重构。
