Sound Structure · 声音结构 · Structure sonore

Sound Protocol · 声音协议

Sound is vibration perceived by living beings. In physics, sound is a mechanical wave; in language, it is a primary medium through which speech becomes perceptible.

在 Unilanguage 中，声音不只是物理现象，也是意义出现之前最早可以被感知的信息形式之一。

Semantic Rules · 语义规则

Vibration · 振动

Sound begins as physical vibration moving through a medium.

声音始于在介质中传播的物理振动。

Perception · 感知

A vibration becomes sound when a listener can perceive it.

振动被听者感知时，才成为经验中的声音。

Memory · 记忆

Memory compares present sound with earlier patterns and contexts.

记忆把当下声音与既有模式和语境进行比较。

Mapping · 映射

Phonetic resemblance may motivate a testable comparison, but does not by itself prove historical relation.

语音相似可以成为可检验比较的起点，但不能单独证明历史同源。

Evidence Boundaries · 证据边界

  1. Document established phonetic and historical facts separately.
  2. Label cross-language sound comparisons as proposed mappings.
  3. Record alternative explanations and limitations.
  4. Treat sound symbolism and deep-history proposals as hypotheses.

Homophony is evidence to investigate, not evidence to conclude. · 同音是调查的入口，而不是结论。
