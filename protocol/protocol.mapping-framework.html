Collanguage / Unilanguage · Research Protocol

Unilanguage Multidimensional Mapping FrameworkUnilanguage 多维映射框架

A research framework for mapping how meaning is discovered, created, transformed, represented, and evaluated across sound, language, writing, culture, space, image, and time.一个研究意义如何在声音、语言、文字、文化、空间、图像与时间之中被发现、创造、转换、表达和评估的框架。

Meaning and structure can survive language change.意义和结构可以穿越语言的变化而继续存在。

1. Three Core Mechanisms三大核心机制

The framework distinguishes three operations. They may interact in one case, but they should not be confused.本框架区分三种操作。它们可以在同一案例中互动，但不应彼此混淆。

1.1 Discovery发现

Discovery identifies relations that may already exist in linguistic, cognitive, historical, cultural, or symbolic data.“发现”识别语言、认知、历史、文化或符号资料中可能已经存在的关系。

Initial Consonant Semantic Field首辅音语义场

Test whether recurring initial consonants correlate with semantic tendencies across controlled datasets.在受控资料集中检验反复出现的首辅音是否与某些语义倾向相关。

Cross-Language Phonetic Mapping跨语言语音映射

Compare sound patterns across languages while controlling for genealogy, borrowing, chance, and reconstruction depth.比较不同语言的声音模式，同时控制谱系、借用、巧合与重构深度。

Vowel Mapping元音映射

Investigate whether vowel features participate in stable or context-dependent sound–meaning associations.研究元音特征是否参与稳定或依赖语境的音义关联。

Chinese Phonetic Components汉字声符

Study phonetic series together with historical readings, semantic radicals, graph development, and character families.结合历史读音、意符、字形演变与汉字家族研究声符系列。

Historical / Etymological Evidence历史／词源证据

Trace attested forms, regular sound change, borrowing paths, written records, and scholarly reconstructions.追踪有文献证明的形式、规律音变、借词路径、文字记录与学术重构。

Cognitive / Spatial Schema认知／空间图式

Map embodied and spatial structures such as container, path, center–periphery, up–down, and near–far.映射容器、路径、中心—边缘、上下、远近等具身与空间结构。

Cross-Cultural Mapping跨文化映射

Compare cultural models without assuming that resemblance proves contact or common origin.比较文化模型，但不假定相似就能证明接触或共同起源。

1.2 Creation创造

Creation produces new relations through literature, poetry, metaphor, story, naming, and culture. These are Created Semantic Relations: their value does not depend on being historical cognates.“创造”通过文学、诗歌、隐喻、故事、命名与文化生成新的关系。这些属于被创造的语义关系；其价值并不依赖它们是否为历史同源词。

  • Literature and poetry as semantic invention.文学与诗歌作为语义发明。
  • Metaphor and story as relation-building systems.隐喻与故事作为关系建构系统。
  • Naming and culture as generators of shared meaning.命名与文化作为共同意义的生成器。

1.3 Transformation转换

Transformation translates a structure from one representational domain into another and tests what is preserved, lost, or newly created.“转换”把结构从一种表达域转入另一种表达域，并检验其中哪些内容被保留、丢失或重新生成。

  • Concrete → Abstract具体 → 抽象
  • Abstract → Concrete抽象 → 具体
  • Motion → Geometry运动 → 几何
  • Concept → Symbol概念 → 符号
  • Concept → Character / Image概念 → 文字／图像
  • Sound → Shape声音 → 形状
  • Language → Gesture语言 → 手势

Transformation network: Language ↔ Character ↔ Symbol ↔ Geometry ↔ Image ↔ Motion转换网络：语言 ↔ 文字 ↔ 符号 ↔ 几何 ↔ 图像 ↔ 运动

2. Diachronic Cross-Language Mapping历时跨语言映射

Comparison must follow historical trajectories, not only present-day resemblance.比较必须追踪历史轨迹，而不能只看现代形式的相似。

English-Side Trajectory英语侧轨迹

  1. Modern English现代英语
  2. Germanic日耳曼语层
  3. Old French古法语层
  4. Latin / Greek拉丁语／希腊语层
  5. PIE reconstruction原始印欧语重构

Chinese-Side Trajectory汉语侧轨迹

  1. Modern Mandarin现代普通话
  2. Middle Chinese中古汉语
  3. Old Chinese上古汉语
  4. Character phonetic series汉字声符系列
  5. Classical semantic usage / dialect evidence古典语义用法／方言证据

Modern forms are only one layer of evidence; historical trajectories may reveal or dissolve apparent correspondences.现代形式只是证据的一层；历史轨迹可能揭示表面对应背后的关系，也可能消解这种对应。

3. Cultural Transmission Layer文化传播层

This layer asks what, if anything, moved between communities and through which medium.这一层追问：究竟有什么在群体之间传播，以及它通过什么媒介传播。

Lexical Transmission词汇传播

Words or morphemes move through contact and borrowing.词或语素通过接触与借用发生传播。

Semantic Transmission语义传播

Meanings, categories, or metaphors move even when the original sound does not.即使原有语音没有传播，意义、范畴或隐喻仍可能传播。

Structural Transmission结构传播

Grammatical, narrative, classificatory, or relational patterns move across systems.语法、叙事、分类或关系模式跨系统传播。

Symbolic Transmission符号传播

Signs, motifs, diagrams, or symbolic conventions move and change form.标记、母题、图式或符号惯例在传播中改变形式。

Independent Convergence独立趋同

Similar constraints or experiences produce similar solutions without transmission.相似的限制或经验在没有传播的情况下产生相似方案。

Relation Origin Test关系起源检验

Every proposed correspondence should be tested against competing explanations.每个被提出的对应关系都应接受相互竞争的解释检验。

  • Common Origin: descent from a shared historical source.共同起源：来自同一个历史来源的继承。
  • Diffusion: transfer through contact, trade, migration, institutions, or texts.传播：通过接触、贸易、迁徙、制度或文本发生转移。
  • Convergence: independent similarity shaped by shared cognitive, bodily, or environmental constraints.趋同：在共同认知、身体或环境限制下独立形成的相似。
  • Coincidence: similarity adequately explained by chance.巧合：可由偶然性充分解释的相似。
  • Unknown: current evidence cannot discriminate among the alternatives.未知：现有证据不足以区分不同解释。

4. Sound Before Writing声音先于文字

This module separates secure knowledge from Unilanguage research questions and from the rule governing interpretation.本模块将较稳固的知识、Unilanguage 的研究问题与解释规则分开。

Layer 1 · Relatively Well-Established Facts第一层 · 较稳固事实

  • Spoken language generally precedes writing historically and developmentally.从历史与个体发展看，口语通常先于文字。
  • Writing systems encode language imperfectly and according to changing conventions.文字系统依据不断变化的惯例，对语言进行不完全编码。
  • Sound change can conceal earlier phonetic relations, while spelling may preserve or distort older layers.音变会遮蔽早期语音关系，而拼写可能保存或扭曲更古老的层次。

Layer 2 · Collanguage / Unilanguage Research Questions第二层 · Collanguage / Unilanguage 研究问题

  • Can homophones or near-homophones expose latent semantic networks within a language?同音或近音形式能否揭示一种语言内部潜在的语义网络？
  • Can phonetic components, dialects, and reconstructed readings reveal older organization hidden by modern characters?声符、方言与重构读音能否揭示被现代汉字遮蔽的早期组织？
  • Which sound–meaning patterns survive statistical, historical, and cross-linguistic controls?哪些音义模式能经受统计、历史和跨语言控制？

Layer 3 · Interpretive Principle第三层 · 解释原则

Homophony is evidence to investigate, not evidence to conclude.同音，是调查的入口，而不是结论。

Homophony or phonetic similarity cannot by itself prove common origin. It can only trigger investigation of historical pronunciation, dialects, phonetic components, semantic networks, regular sound correspondences, chronology, borrowing, and chance.同音或近音本身不能证明同源。它只能触发对古音、方言、声符、语义网络、规律音系对应、年代、借用与巧合的调查。

5. Script, Symbol, and Experimental Hypotheses文字、符号与实验假设

Symbol-Origin Hypothesis符号起源假说

Explore whether recurring symbolic forms may preserve spatial, embodied, technical, or cultural structures. This is an experimental or speculative research layer unless independently supported.探索反复出现的符号形式是否可能保存空间、具身、技术或文化结构。除非有独立证据支持，否则它属于实验或推测性研究层。

Script & Symbol Mapping文字与符号映射

Compare graphic form, writing function, sound value, meaning, gesture, geometry, and historical context without treating visual similarity as proof of relation.比较图形、书写功能、音值、意义、手势、几何和历史语境，但不把视觉相似当作关系证明。

Required Research Record必需研究记录

  1. Established facts — dated, attributable observations.已确立事实——可注明年代与来源的观察。
  2. Supported interpretation — an inference backed by converging evidence.有支持的解释——获得多项证据共同支持的推论。
  3. Experimental hypothesis — a testable proposal with explicit variables.实验性假设——具有明确变量、可以检验的提案。
  4. Speculative extension — a lower-confidence extrapolation kept separate from findings.推测性延伸——与研究发现分开标注的低置信度外推。
  5. Falsification test — the observation or result that would weaken or reject the proposal.证伪检验——能够削弱或否定该提案的观察或结果。

A hypothesis does not need to be established before it can be tested computationally. Its status must be explicit.一个假设不必先成为定论，才可以接受计算检验；但它的状态必须明确。

Unilanguage does not wait for certainty; it engineers uncertainty into testable structures.Unilanguage 不等待确定性；它把不确定性工程化为可检验的结构。

7. Evidence Status and Mapping Level证据状态与映射层级

Evidence Status证据状态

  • Established: broadly accepted or directly documented.已确立：获得广泛接受或有直接记录。
  • Supported: backed by multiple relevant lines of evidence.有支持：获得多条相关证据支持。
  • Experimental: formulated for structured testing.实验性：已被结构化，以便接受检验。
  • Speculative: exploratory and presently low-confidence.推测性：处于探索阶段，当前置信度较低。
  • Cultural: meaningful within a cultural, literary, or symbolic context, without implying historical descent.文化性：在文化、文学或符号语境中有意义，但不意味着历史继承关系。

Mapping Level A / B / C / D映射层级 A／B／C／D

  • A — Direct: documented or reproducible correspondence.A—直接：有文献记录或可重复验证的对应。
  • B — Supported: converging evidence with remaining uncertainty.B—支持：证据趋于一致，但仍存在不确定性。
  • C — Experimental: testable pattern requiring further data or controls.C—实验：需要更多资料或控制条件的可检验模式。
  • D — Speculative / Creative: exploratory, symbolic, or intentionally created relation.D—推测／创造：探索性、符号性或有意创造的关系。

An Evidence Status records epistemic confidence; a Mapping Level records the strength and kind of correspondence. They should be assigned separately.“证据状态”记录认知置信度；“映射层级”记录对应关系的强度与类型。两者应分别标注。

8. Long-Term Mission长期使命

  1. Discover shared semantic structures among human languages.发现人类语言之间共享的语义结构。
  2. Build a language-independent semantic protocol.建立不依赖某一种自然语言的语义协议。
  3. Explore a universal symbolic writing system for humans, AI, and potentially interstellar communication.探索一种面向人类、人工智能，并可能用于星际交流的通用符号书写系统。
