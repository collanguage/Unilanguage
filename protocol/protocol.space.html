01

Definition · What Is Space? · 定义：什么是空间？

English

Space is a relational field in which an entity, event or idea can be located, oriented, bounded, separated, connected or moved.

Physical space concerns observable relations among bodies and places. Semantic space concerns structured relations through which ideas are understood.

中文

空间是一种关系场；实体、事件或观念可以在其中被定位、定向、设定边界、分离、连接或移动。

物理空间涉及身体与地点之间可观察的关系；语义空间涉及帮助人们理解观念的结构性关系。

Protocol position · 协议定位： SPACE is a theory chapter in the Protocol Book, not a named operation in the current Semantic Protocol. It explains spatial foundations used by operations such as MOVE, EXIST, UP, DOWN and CONNECT. · SPACE 是 Protocol Book 中的理论章节，不是当前 Semantic Protocol 的正式操作名；它解释 MOVE、EXIST、UP、DOWN 与 CONNECT 等操作的空间基础。

02

Spatial Direction · 空间方向

S1

Above / Below · 上／下

Vertical relations organize height, power, aspiration and grounding.

垂直关系可以组织高度、权力、追求与立足等意义。

S2

Inside / Outside · 内／外

Containment distinguishes membership, privacy, protection and exclusion.

容纳关系区分归属、私密、保护与排除。

S3

Near / Far · 近／远

Distance structures accessibility, intimacy, attention and relevance.

距离可以组织可接近性、亲密度、注意力与相关性。

S4

Toward / Away · 趋近／离开

Direction provides a path for physical and abstract change.

方向为物理变化和抽象变化提供路径。

03

Spatial Relations · 空间关系

S5

Boundary · 边界

A boundary creates an inside and an outside. Crossing it changes the semantic state.

边界产生内与外；跨越边界会改变语义状态。

S6

Containment · 容纳

A container may protect, limit, include or conceal what it holds.

容器可以保护、限制、包含或隐藏其中之物。

S7

Path · 路径

Movement can be represented as SOURCE → PATH → GOAL.

移动可以表示为“来源 → 路径 → 目标”。

S8

Center / Edge · 中心／边缘

Position may organize importance, visibility and social membership.

位置可以组织重要性、可见性与社会归属。

04

Spatial Metaphor · 空间隐喻

English

Human beings repeatedly use bodily and spatial experience to understand non-spatial domains: high status, deep thought, close relations, distant memory, entering a profession and leaving a state.

中文

人类反复借助身体经验和空间经验理解非空间领域，例如地位高、思想深、关系近、记忆遥远、进入职业以及离开某种状态。

PHYSICAL RELATION → CONCEPTUAL STRUCTURE → LINGUISTIC EXPRESSION
物理关系 → 概念结构 → 语言表达

05

Space and Meaning · 空间与意义

English

Spatial relations do not explain every meaning. They form one important and testable layer for constructing, comparing and translating meaning. Context determines which spatial features are active.

中文

空间关系不能解释所有意义，但它为意义的建构、比较和翻译提供了一个重要且可检验的层级。具体语境决定哪些空间特征被激活。

07

Applied Case: Sky · 应用案例：Sky

English

Sky activates ABOVE and OPEN SPACE in ordinary use. Literary and cultural contexts may additionally activate COVER, VAULT, BOUNDARY, FREEDOM, FATE or HEAVEN.

The Collanguage hypothesis sky ↔ 盖 tests whether phonetic similarity and a shared cover metaphor can support a cross-language semantic mapping.

中文

Sky 在日常用法中激活 ABOVE（上方）与 OPEN SPACE（开放空间）。在文学和文化语境中，还可能激活 COVER（覆盖）、VAULT（穹隆）、BOUNDARY（边界）、FREEDOM（自由）、FATE（命运）或 HEAVEN（天界）。

Collanguage 的 sky ↔ 盖 假说检验：语音相似与共同的“覆盖”隐喻能否共同支持跨语言语义映射。

The protocol authorizes a comparison; it does not prove historical cognacy. · 协议允许建立可检验的比较，但不能因此证明历史同源。
