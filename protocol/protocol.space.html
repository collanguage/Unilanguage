01

Literary Introduction · 文学引言

English

A child looks upward. Above him, the sky appears without walls, yet it gathers the whole world beneath it. A bird rises, a star seems distant, and a cloud passes from east to west. Before the child learns the words height, distance, or freedom, the body has already understood their directions.

中文

一个孩子抬头望去。天空没有墙，却把整个世界收在它的下面。 鸟向上飞，星辰显得遥远，云从东方移向西方。在孩子学会 “高度”、“距离”或“自由”这些词以前，身体已经先理解了 它们的方向。

English

Language begins in lived relations: above and below, inside and outside, near and far, toward and away. This theory chapter studies how these relations become semantic structure.

中文

语言开始于身体所经历的关系：上与下、内与外、近与远、 趋近与离开。本理论章节研究这些关系如何成为语义结构。

02

Definition · What Is Space? · 定义：什么是空间？

English

In ordinary use, space may mean room, distance, an interval, an area, or the expanse beyond Earth. In this Protocol Book theory chapter, space is defined as a relational field.

中文

在通常用法中，空间可以指余地、距离、 间隔、区域，或地球之外的广阔宇宙。在本协议书理论章节中， 空间被定义为一种关系场。

Space is a relational field in which an entity, event, or idea can be located, oriented, bounded, separated, connected, or moved.

空间是一种关系场；实体、事件或观念可以在其中被定位、 确定方向、划定边界、分离、连接或移动。

English · Two layers

Physical space

Observable relations among bodies and places: position, distance, direction, boundary, containment, and movement.

Semantic space

A structured field in which ideas are understood through relations such as high/low, inner/outer, near/far, center/edge, and source/path/goal.

中文 · 两个层面

物理空间

身体与地点之间可观察的关系，包括位置、距离、方向、 边界、容纳和移动。

语义空间

一种结构化的关系场；观念可以通过高/低、内/外、近/远、 中心/边缘以及来源/路径/目标等关系被理解。

English

This chapter does not claim that all meanings are spatial. It proposes that spatial relations provide one important and testable layer for constructing, comparing, and translating meaning.

中文

本章并不主张所有意义都是空间性的，而是提出：空间关系 为意义的构造、比较和翻译提供了一个重要且可以检验的层面。

Positioning statement

Position in the protocol

SPACE is not a named operation in the current Semantic Protocol. This chapter explains the spatial foundations of existing operations such as MOVE, CHANGE, EXIST, UP, DOWN, and CONNECT.

定位说明

在协议中的位置

SPACE 不是当前 Semantic Protocol 中的一项 正式操作。本章解释 MOVE、 CHANGE、EXIST、 UP、DOWN 和 CONNECT 等现有操作的空间基础。

04

Etymology · 词源

English and French · 英语与法语

English explanation

English space entered through Old French espace, from Latin spatium. The Latin word carried senses including room, area, distance, and an interval of time. French espace belongs to the same historical line. The deeper origin of spatium is uncertain.

中文解释

英语 space 经古法语 espace 进入英语，而 espace 源自拉丁语 spatium。这个拉丁词曾表示余地、 区域、距离以及一段时间。现代法语 espace 也属于同一历史词源链。 拉丁语 spatium 更深层的来源尚不确定。

Chinese · 汉语

English explanation

Chinese 空间 combines 空 (“empty, unoccupied, open space”) and 间 (“interval, between, room”). The compound can therefore be understood both as an available place that may contain something and as a relational interval between things.

中文解释

汉语“空间”由“空”与“间”组成。“空”可以表示 未被占据、没有内容或可容纳事物的空处；“间” 突出事物之间的间隔、房间或关系。因此，“空间” 既可被理解为可容纳事物之处，也可被理解为事物 之间的关系场。

Evidence boundary

The relation between English space and French espace is etymological. The comparison with Chinese 空间 is semantic and conceptual. This page does not claim historical cognacy between them.

证据边界

英语 space 与法语 espace 具有历史词源关系；它们与汉语“空间”的比较属于语义和 概念比较。本页并不声称英语、法语词与汉语词之间存在 已证实的历史同源关系。

05

Unilanguage Interpretation · 共通语言解释

English

Unilanguage treats space as a semantic coordinate system. A word or sentence may place an entity within a field, assign it a direction, establish a boundary, or describe a path of change.

中文

Unilanguage 把空间视为一种 语义坐标系统。一个词或句子可以把实体 放在某个关系场中，为它确定方向、建立边界，或描述一条 变化路径。

Sound · 声音 → Spatial orientation · 空间定向 → Conceptual relation · 概念关系 → Meaning · 意义 → Language and translation · 语言与翻译

Example

Rise first describes upward movement. It can then describe a rise in temperature, status, hope, or intensity. The physical direction remains available as a semantic pattern even when no object literally moves.

例子

Rise 首先描述向上的移动，随后也可描述温度、 地位、希望或强度的上升。即使没有物体发生实际移动， 物理方向仍然可以作为一种语义模式保留下来。

Spatial Metaphors · 空间隐喻

UP · 上

Increase, emergence, visibility, power, or hope.

增长、出现、可见、力量或希望。

DOWN · 下

Decrease, descent, submission, concealment, or sadness.

减少、下降、服从、隐藏或悲伤。

IN · 内

Containment, belonging, privacy, memory, or inward attention.

容纳、归属、隐私、记忆或向内的注意。

OUT · 外

Expression, exclusion, expansion, appearance, or release.

表达、排除、扩展、显现或释放。

Research boundary

These associations are analytical possibilities, not fixed universal equations. IN, OUT, TOWARD, and AWAY describe spatial relations here; they are not being added as formal operations.

研究边界

这些关联是分析上的可能性，并非固定的普遍公式。本章中的 IN、OUT、 TOWARD 和 AWAY 只描述空间关系，并未被加入 Semantic Protocol 作为正式操作。

06

Spatial Analysis Rules · 空间分析规则

English

These rules guide analysis within this theory chapter. They do not create a new SPACE operation or expand the official Semantic Protocol.

中文

以下规则用于指导本理论章节中的分析。它们不会创设新的 SPACE 操作，也不会自动扩展正式的 Semantic Protocol。

  1. Position · 位置

    Meaning may locate an entity above, below, within, outside, before, behind, near, or far from a reference point.

    意义可以把实体定位在参照点的上、下、内、外、前、后、近处或远处。

  2. Direction · 方向

    UP and DOWN are current operations; inside, outside, toward, and away remain analytical relations.

    UP 与 DOWN 是现有操作；内、外、趋近与离开仍属于分析关系。

  3. Boundary · 边界

    A boundary creates an inside and an outside. Crossing, opening, closing, entering, or leaving it changes the semantic state.

    边界产生内与外；跨越、打开、关闭、进入或离开边界都会改变语义状态。

  4. Path · 路径

    Movement can be represented as SOURCE → PATH → GOAL in physical and abstract processes.

    在物理过程和抽象过程中，移动都可以表示为 来源 → 路径 → 目标。

  5. Scale and Distance · 尺度与距离

    Near/far, large/small, center/edge, and open/closed may organize attention, intimacy, importance, and accessibility.

    近/远、大/小、中心/边缘以及开/闭可以组织注意、亲密、重要性与可接近性。

  6. Metaphorical Projection · 隐喻投射

    A spatial relation may be projected onto a non-spatial domain; translation should preserve the intended relation.

    空间关系可以投射到非空间领域；翻译应尽可能保留原有的关系意图。

  7. Context Verification · 语境检验

    A mapping must be checked against syntax, usage, culture, and speaker intention rather than accepted from form alone.

    映射不能只凭形式成立，必须结合句法、用法、文化和说话者意图进行检验。

Research hypothesis

The project is testing whether recurring sound patterns interact with spatial meaning—for example, a as outward, i/u as downward, o as inward, and tonal movement as spatial movement. These are not universally proven linguistic laws.

研究假设

本项目正在检验反复出现的声音模式是否会与空间意义互动， 例如把 a 解释为向外，把 i/u 解释为向下，把 o 解释为向内，以及把声调运动理解为空间运动。这些并非已被 普遍证实的语言学定律。

09

Reflection · 思考

English

When people say that hope rises, a thought comes from within, a problem lies ahead, or someone falls into despair, language turns invisible experience into a place that can be entered, crossed, shared, or escaped.

中文

当我们说“希望升起”、“思想来自内心”、“问题就在前方” 或“陷入绝望”时，语言把看不见的经验变成了一个可以进入、 穿越、分享或离开的地方。

Philosophical interpretation

Speech may do more than report an already arranged world. Through relations, boundaries, viewpoints, and directions, it may construct a temporary semantic world that speaker and listener inhabit together. This is a philosophical interpretation, not historical or experimental proof.

哲学解释

言语可能不只是报告一个已经安排好的世界。通过关系、 边界、视点和方向，它也可能建造一个说话者与听者共同 进入的临时语义世界。这属于哲学解释，而不是历史证据 或实验证明。
