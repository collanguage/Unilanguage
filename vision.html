Our Starting Question · 我们的起点

What shared structures might exist among human languages? We compare English, Chinese, French and other languages through sound, meaning, concepts and culture. We distinguish linguistic evidence from testable hypotheses and imaginative associations.

人类不同语言之间，是否存在某些共同结构？我们从英语、汉语、法语及其他语言的语音、语义、概念和文化出发进行比较，严格区分语言学证据、可检验假说与创造性联想。

Five Horizons · 五个发展阶段

01 Discover · 发现

Explore possible cross-language semantic structures and document counterexamples.

探索跨语言语义结构，记录支持证据与反例。

02 Learn · 学习

Apply multilingual comparisons, sound, imagery and cultural context to language learning.

把多语言比较、声音、图像和文化语境应用于语言学习。

03 Co-create · 共创

Invite learners to propose mappings, interpretations and symbols, subject to review.

邀请学习者提出映射、解释与符号，经讨论和审核后积累知识。

04 Evolve · 演化

Explore a language-independent semantic protocol and possible forms of common expression.

探索独立于单一自然语言的语义协议与共同表达形式。

05 Beyond Earth · 走向太空

Investigate potential future communication needs of space stations, lunar settlements and Martian cities. This is a long-term vision, not a demonstrated application.

探索未来空间站、月球定居点与火星城市的交流需求。这是远期愿景，而非已经证实的应用。

Linguistic Heritage & Humanistic Exchange · 语言遗产与人文交流

Every human language carries a unique way of understanding the world. From classical texts to living dialects, from languages spoken across China to those of Eurasia and beyond, linguistic traditions preserve memories, knowledge and cultural imagination.

Unilanguage seeks not only to explore shared meanings but also to cherish differences. Through learning, research and collaboration, we hope that lesser-known expressions may be documented and rediscovered, and that people can share their understanding of the world.

Our goal is not to replace linguistic diversity with uniformity, but to build common understanding through diversity.

每一种人类语言，都承载着一种独特的理解世界的方式。从古代典籍到今天仍在使用的方言，从中国各民族语言到欧亚大陆乃至世界各地的语言，每一种语言都保存着人类的记忆、知识与文化想象。

Unilanguage 不仅希望探索不同语言之间的共通意义，也希望珍视它们各自的独特性。通过学习、研究与协作，让鲜为人知的表达得到记录和重新发现，让不同文化背景的人分享对世界的理解。

我们的目标不是以统一取代多样性，而是在多样性之中建立共同理解。

Space Common Language Initiative · 航天共通语言计划

To enable humanity to carry its diverse languages and cultures into space, while developing a shared capacity for mutual understanding.

Our long-term research connects semantic protocols, multilingual learning, linguistic heritage, symbolic expression and human–AI communication.

让人类带着各自的语言与文化走向太空，同时拥有共同理解彼此的能力。

这项长期研究将语义协议、跨语言学习、语言遗产、符号表达与未来人机交流连接起来。

Research Integrity · 研究原则

An entry may be published; a hypothesis must be graded; literature may explore freely; evidence must be evaluated independently.

Publication is not proof of historical linguistic relationship. Learning contributions remain proposals until reviewed.

词条可以发表，假说必须标级，文学可以自由展开，证据必须独立核验。

发表不等于证明历史同源；学习者的贡献在审核前仍属于提案。
