Evidence & References · 证据与引文

Established Evidence · 已确立证据

State externally established factual claims with inline numbered citations, for example [1].

Cultural Sources | 文化资料 (optional · 可选)

Cite verified mythology records, literature, songs, religious texts, folklore or ethnographic sources beside the cultural claim they support. State whether they document an association, usage, semantic change or lexicalization.

Unilanguage Analysis / Proposed Mapping · Unilanguage 分析／拟议映射

State the proposed mapping, mapping level, hypothesis status, limitations and alternatives. Do not present it as established etymology.

Required distinction · 必须区分： Historical / Etymological Evidence ≠ Cultural Association. A cultural example may document semantic association, but it does not by itself prove historical etymology or common origin. Verify specific mythological or religious claims before presenting them as external evidence.

References · 参考文献

  1. Author or institution. Title. Edition or publication, year. Stable URL and access date when applicable.
