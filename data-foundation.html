Schema v1.0 · Four independent status axes

Entry · 词条

Draft / Reviewed / Published describes editorial availability only.

Mapping / Hypothesis · 映射／假说

Candidate / Reviewed / Supported / Rejected and A–D level are graded independently.

Evidence / History · 证据／历史

Historical, Phonetic-Semantic, Cognitive and Speculative tracks each carry their own status and confidence.

Literature · 文学

Prose, poetry, translations and manuscripts remain publishable and searchable, but are never historical evidence.

Editorial policy · 编辑原则： An entry may be published; a hypothesis must be graded; literature may explore freely; evidence must be evaluated independently. · 词条可以发表，假说必须标级，文学可以自由展开，证据必须独立核验。

Package G.1 · Seven independent layers

Mapping is not etymology · 映射不等于词源

Each English word has exactly one primary Chinese mapping. Author rationale, historical lineage and unclassified notes are separate objects.

Hypothesis is not result · 假说不等于结果

Sound, consonant and letter-symbol hypotheses carry their own status/evidence/confidence. Experiments cite hypotheses and retain their tested condition and result.

Review per object · 逐对象审核

Source Verification and AI Review are separate for every mapping, rationale, etymology, hypothesis, note and experiment.
