Hypothesis Lab

假说实验室 · Engineering uncertainty into testable structures

Version 1.1 · Methodology and Three Active Records · 方法论与三条正式记录 · 2026-08-18

Purpose

Hypothesis Lab registers hypotheses that extend beyond their existing historical or etymological evidence: testable but unconfirmed sound–meaning regularities, and high-risk deep-history proposals concerning cross-civilizational transmission, remote diffusion or common symbolic origins. A claim may enter before it is established, but its evidence, limits, review state and falsification test must remain visible.

目的

假说实验室收纳已有部分历史或词源证据、但进一步延伸的假说；可检验但尚未确认的声音—语义规律；以及关于跨文明传播、远古扩散或符号同源的高风险深历史假说。一个主张可以在尚未确立时进入系统，但其证据、边界、评审状态与证伪条件必须始终清楚可见。

Responsibility Boundary · 职责边界

Protocol Book · 协议书Defines rules and evidence structures. · 定义规则与证据结构。 Language Book · 语言书Preserves word entries and documented cases. · 保存词条与已有案例。

01

Operating Principles · 运行原则

Homophony is evidence to investigate, not evidence to conclude.
同音是值得调查的证据，不是可以直接下结论的证据。
A hypothesis does not need to be established before it can be tested computationally. Its status must be explicit.
一个假说不必先成为定论，才有资格进入计算和工程测试；但其证据状态必须明确。
Unilanguage does not wait for certainty; it engineers uncertainty into testable structures.
Unilanguage 不等待确定性，而是把不确定性工程化为可检验的结构。
Admission rule · 收录规则： Non-mainstream hypotheses may enter the system, but they must be labeled by evidence status and must never be presented as established etymology without historical evidence. · 非主流假说可以进入系统，但必须标注证据状态；没有历史证据时，不得写成已确立词源。

02

Four Evidence Tracks · 四条证据轨道

Track 1

Historical Relation · 历史关系

Inheritance, borrowing, attested transmission, dated forms and documented sound change.

继承、借用、有记录的传播、可断代形式与有文献的音变。

Track 2

Phonetic-Semantic Tendency · 语音—语义倾向

A sound–meaning association tested against controlled, genealogical and chance baselines.

把语音—语义关联与受控、谱系及随机基线进行比较。

Track 3

Cognitive Association · 认知关联

Embodiment, perception, spatial schema or conceptual metaphor that may motivate a mapping.

可能促成映射的具身、感知、空间图式或概念隐喻。

Track 4

Speculative Deep-History Hypothesis · 远古历史假说

A distant proposed relation that must be distinguishable from convergence, borrowing and chance.

必须能够与趋同、借用和巧合相区分的远古关系提案。

03

Hypothesis Status · 假说状态

01

Established facts · 已确立事实

Documented observations or historical relations accepted independently of the larger hypothesis.

不依赖更大假说、已有文献支持或独立确认的观察与历史关系。

02

Supported interpretation · 有支持的解释

An interpretation supported by converging evidence, while alternatives and limits remain visible.

获得多项证据支持、但仍保留替代解释与适用边界的解释。

03

Experimental hypothesis · 实验性假说

A specified claim ready for computational or engineering tests but not yet confirmed.

已经明确陈述、可进入计算或工程测试、但尚未确认的主张。

04

Speculative extension · 推测性延伸

A higher-risk extension beyond the evidence currently supporting the core claim.

超出现有核心证据范围、风险更高的延伸主张。

05

Falsification test · 证伪检验

The observations or results that would require the claim to be narrowed, downgraded, split or rejected.

能够促使主张缩小、降级、拆分或否定的观察与结果。

04

Initial Categories · 首批分类

Sound Hypotheses · 声音假说

M / M–N / Vowels / GKH / V

Controlled tests of recurring sound–meaning tendencies, including M—measurement—moon, M–N correspondence, vowel directionality, the G/K/H comparison group and V-related transformation proposals.

对反复出现的音义倾向进行受控检验，包括 M—measurement—moon、M–N 对应、元音方向性、G／K／H 比较组与 V 相关转换提案。

Script Hypotheses · 文字假说

Form, component and writing function · 字形、部件与书写功能

Tests of graphic form, phonetic components and possible semantic extensions without treating visual similarity as proof.

检验字形、声符及可能的语义延伸，但不把视觉相似当作证明。

Deep-History Hypotheses · 深历史假说

Sound before attested writing · 现存文字以前的声音

Proposals about sound–meaning layers older than surviving scripts, with explicit null models and deep-time limitations.

研究早于现存文字的音义层，同时明确零模型与深时限制。

Cognitive Hypotheses · 认知假说

Embodiment and conceptual structure · 具身与概念结构

Cross-language patterns that may arise from shared perception, action, space or metaphor rather than common ancestry.

可能源于共同感知、行动、空间或隐喻，而非共同祖源的跨语言模式。

Transformation Hypotheses · 转换假说

Meaning across representational forms · 跨表达形态的意义

Tests of which semantic relations survive transformation among word, sound, script, geometry, image and motion.

检验意义在词、声音、文字、几何、图像与运动之间转换时，哪些语义关系得以保留。

05

UNI-SOUND-M-001 · M—Measurement—Moon

Evidence Status · 证据状态 Supported Core + Experimental Extension The inherited moon–month relationship belongs to the supported historical core. Measure, meter, mete, mode, meal, mile and medicine are extension candidates only: they have different lexical histories and do not establish one M-centered etymological family. · moon—month 的继承关系属于有支持的历史核心；measure、meter、mete、mode、meal、mile、medicine 只属于延伸候选：它们具有不同的词汇历史，不能据此确立一个以 M 为中心的共同词源家族。

Known Evidence · 已知证据

Supported Core · 有支持的核心

moon ↔ month

English moon and month belong to a documented inherited Germanic word family. Historical dictionary evidence connects Old English mōna “moon” with mōnath “month,” reflecting the long-standing use of lunar cycles to organize time.

Historical Relation · Established / Supported

Reference: Merriam-Webster dictionary histories for moon and month. The reference supports the lexical relationship; it does not support the larger M semantic-field claim.

Experimental Extension · 实验性延伸

M → measure / meter / mete / mode / meal / mile / medicine?

实验性主张是：M 首音可能反复呈现与测量、循环、数量、边界或时间相关的语义倾向。measure、meter、mete、mode、meal、mile、medicine 等词只能作为待检验的候选样本；它们与 moon／month 的有据历史关系必须严格分开，也不能自行证明共同来源或普遍语义规律。

Review Status · 评审状态Core documented; extension author-proposed; corpus design, external review and replication pending. · 核心已有文献；延伸由作者提出；语料设计、外部评审与复现待完成。
Falsification Test · 证伪检验If M-initial words are not significantly concentrated in the measure / cycle / quantity / boundary / time semantic fields, or if an apparent concentration is mainly produced by a few cognate families, the hypothesis must be downgraded, split or rejected. UNI-SOUND-MN-001 · M–N Correspondence Hypothesis · M–N 对应假说

Evidence Status · 证据状态 Experimental · 实验性 Registered for controlled testing within Collanguage / Unilanguage. A small number of attractive examples cannot establish a general sound law. · 登记为 Collanguage / Unilanguage 内部的受控检验项目；少量引人注意的案例不能建立普遍音变规律。

Primary observation · 核心观察

man /mæn/ ↔ 男 nán

The forms show a strong semantic overlap and a compact syllable resemblance. The proposed initial m ↔ n relation is experimental; no historical relationship is inferred.

Open Mapping Score v0.2 Case 03 · 打开第三案例 →

Secondary observation · 辅助观察

man ↔ 氓 méng / máng

词首 m、近似元音与词尾鼻音 n↔ŋ 构成可检验的结构相似；但表示“百姓”的读音主要是 méng，更近音的 máng 主要见于“流氓”。这项错位必须计入反证与限制。

Hypothesis ID · 假说编号UNI-SOUND-MN-001
Title · 标题M–N Correspondence Hypothesis｜M–N 对应假说
Author · 作者Jinkai Liu｜劉金凱
System · 系统Collanguage / Unilanguage
Claim · 主张In specified cross-language comparison sets, bilabial nasal /m/ and coronal nasal /n/ may participate in recurring phonetic-semantic correspondences more often than matched baselines predict. This is a statistical research question, not a historical sound law. · 在预先规定的跨语言比较集中，双唇鼻音 /m/ 与舌冠鼻音 /n/ 可能以高于匹配基线的频率参与反复出现的音义对应。这是统计研究问题，不是历史音变定律。
Supporting Cases · 支持案例Primary candidate: man /mæn/ ↔ 男 nán, with high semantic overlap and whole-syllable resemblance. Secondary candidate: man ↔ 氓 méng / máng, where sound and sense support different readings and must remain separated. Future cases must be pre-registered rather than selected after inspection. · 主候选为 man↔男；辅助候选为 man↔氓，但其音义支持落在不同读音上，必须分开。未来案例须预注册，不能事后挑选。
Counterexamples · 反例Most m- and n-initial words do not share person/male meanings; many person/male words have neither sound; similar short nasal syllables are common; unrelated languages can converge by chance. The méng/máng split in 氓 weakens any unqualified form-meaning match. · 多数 m／n 首音词不共享“人／男性”义，大量“人／男性”词也没有这些声音；短鼻音音节常见，非亲属语言可偶然趋同；氓的 méng／máng 分读削弱任何不加限定的音义匹配。
Test Method · 测试方法Pre-register languages, semantic domains, transcription rules, alignment criteria and exclusions. Compare m↔n matches against other nasal and non-nasal pairs while controlling genealogy, borrowing, phonotactic frequency, word length and multiple comparisons. Use independent semantic coders and a held-out replication set. · 预注册语言、语义域、转写、对齐与排除规则；以其他鼻音及非鼻音配对为对照，控制谱系、借用、音系频率、词长与多重比较，并使用独立语义编码者及留出复现集。
Falsification Test · 证伪检验Narrow, downgrade or reject the hypothesis if m↔n pairings do not exceed matched baselines, disappear after genealogical and borrowing controls, depend on post-hoc semantic categories, or fail independent replication. · 若 m↔n 配对不能超过匹配基线、控制谱系与借用后消失、依赖事后语义分类，或不能独立复现，则缩小、降级或否定假说。

07

UNI-VOWEL-DIRECTION-001 · Vowel Directionality Hypothesis · 元音方向性假说

Evidence Status · 证据状态 Experimental / Cultural · 实验性／文化性 Articulatory and acoustic tendencies are tested separately from yin–yang interpretation. · 发音／声学倾向与阴阳文化解释分别检验。
Articulatory / acoustic proposal · 发音／声学提案a → open / outward candidate

Open jaw posture and lower-vowel acoustics may motivate perceived expansion or outwardness in some tasks. This tendency is not assumed to be universal. · 较开放的口腔姿态与低元音声学特征，可能在部分任务中促成“扩张／向外”感知，但不预设其普遍性。

Directional contrast · 方向对比i / u / o → inward, closed or other tendencies?

High, rounded or back vowels may pattern differently by articulation, acoustics, language and task. They must not be collapsed into one “inward” class before testing. · 高元音、圆唇元音与后元音可能因发音、声学、语言和任务呈现不同模式；检验前不得统并为单一“向内”类别。

Cultural interpretation · 文化解释a → 阳; i / u / o → 阴?

Yin–yang labels are a Collanguage cultural-philosophical interpretation of perceived direction. They are not equivalent to acoustic measurements and do not constitute established linguistic law. · 阴阳标签是 Collanguage 对感知方向的文化／哲学解释，不等同于声学测量，也不是已确立语言规律。

Scope · 范围Perception, production and symbolism

The proposal may be tested in articulation, acoustic space, forced-choice perception, nonce-word meaning and corpus association as distinct experiments. · 可分别在发音、声学空间、强迫选择感知、无意义词配义与语料关联中测试。

Hypothesis ID · 假说编号UNI-VOWEL-DIRECTION-001
Title · 标题Vowel Directionality Hypothesis｜元音方向性假说
Author · 作者Jinkai Liu｜劉金凱
System · 系统Collanguage / Unilanguage
Claim · 主张Vowel articulation and acoustics may bias perceived spatial direction or openness: a is proposed as an outward/open candidate, while i, u, o may show inward, closed or other directional tendencies that require separate specification. Yin–yang is a cultural interpretive layer, not the physical claim itself. · 元音的发音与声学特征可能影响对空间方向或开合的感知：a 是向外／开放候选，i、u、o 可能呈向内、闭合或其他方向倾向，须分别明确；阴阳属于文化解释层，不等于物理层主张。
Supporting Cases · 支持案例User-origin observations: a as outward / 阳; o, u as inward / 阴; i and other vowels as possible downward, closed or contrasting directions. The man ↔ 男 case records an open-vowel resemblance but does not score yin–yang interpretation. · 用户原始观察包括 a→向外／阳，o、u→向内／阴，i 等可能表示向下、闭合或对比方向；man↔男记录开放元音相似，但阴阳解释不计分。
Counterexamples · 反例The same vowel carries many unrelated meanings; vowel realization varies across languages and speakers; roundedness, height, backness, duration, tone, coarticulation and orthography may produce conflicting directional judgments. · 同一元音承载大量无关意义；不同语言与说话者的元音实现不同；圆唇、高低、前后、时长、声调、协同发音与拼写都可能造成冲突判断。
Test Method · 测试方法Define acoustic and articulatory variables before cultural labels. Run preregistered perception and nonce-word experiments across languages; randomize symbols and order; compare within- and cross-cultural groups; model vowels separately and report null results. Test yin–yang labeling as a distinct cultural coding task. · 在文化标签之前定义声学与发音变量；跨语言开展预注册感知与无意义词实验，随机化符号和顺序，比较文化内外群体，分别建模各元音并报告零结果；阴阳标签另作文化编码任务。
Falsification Test · 证伪检验Narrow or reject a directional subclaim if effects fail preregistered thresholds, reverse across replications without a predicted moderator, vanish when orthography and culture are controlled, or cannot distinguish the target vowel from matched alternatives. · 若效应达不到预注册阈值、在无预测调节因素时跨复现反转、控制拼写与文化后消失，或不能把目标元音与匹配对照区分，则缩小或否定相应方向子主张。
Mapping Type · 映射类型Phonetic-Semantic Tendency + Cognitive / Cultural Association · 语音—语义倾向＋认知／文化关联
Evidence Status · 证据状态Experimental / Cultural · 实验性／文化性
Review Status · 评审状态Registered; operational definitions and experiments pending. · 已登记；操作性定义与实验待完成。

Layer boundary · 分层边界： A measurable articulatory or acoustic tendency would not, by itself, validate a yin–yang interpretation; a meaningful yin–yang interpretation would not, by itself, establish a universal phonetic law. · 可测量的发音／声学倾向本身不能证实阴阳解释；有意义的阴阳解释本身也不能建立普遍语音规律。
