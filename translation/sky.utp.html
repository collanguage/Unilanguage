Context Tests · 语境测试

Source · 原句 Recommended translation · 推荐译文 Reason · 理由

05

Mapping Evaluation: sky ↔ 盖 · 映射评估

English

The hypothesis combines two forms of evidence: partial phonetic resemblance and the semantic metaphor SKY AS COVER. It is stronger than a sound-only association but remains insufficient for a historical cognacy claim.

中文

该假说结合两类证据：部分语音相似，以及“天空如覆盖物”的语义隐喻。它比单纯的声音联想更有结构，但仍不足以证明历史同源。

Dimension · 维度 Observation · 观察 Score · 评分
Literal equivalence · 字面对应 盖 is not the ordinary dictionary equivalent of sky · “盖”不是 sky 的日常词典对应 1 / 5
Semantic structure · 语义结构 ABOVE–COVER–BOUNDARY relation is coherent · 上方—覆盖—边界关系连贯 4 / 5
Phonetic resemblance · 语音相似 /aɪ/ and velar stops are comparable; /s/, voicing and tone are unmatched · /aɪ/ 与软腭塞音可比；/s/、清浊和声调未对应 3 / 5
Historical evidence · 历史证据 independent documented histories; no demonstrated contact chain · 各有独立历史；未证明传播链 0 / 5
Cultural explanatory value · 文化解释价值 supported by Gaitian and literary cover imagery · 可由盖天说和文学覆盖意象说明 4 / 5

UTP result · 评测结论： Experimental, medium semantic similarity · 实验性，中等语义相似度

Evidence Layers · 证据分层

Lexical evidence · 词汇证据

sky → 天空 is established as an ordinary translation.

sky → 天空 是已经确立的日常翻译。

Etymological evidence · 词源证据

English sky and Chinese 盖 have separate documented histories.

英语 sky 与汉语“盖”各自拥有独立的文献历史。

Semantic evidence · 语义证据

Cover and vault metaphors provide a testable conceptual relation.

覆盖与穹隆隐喻提供可检验的概念关系。

Cultural evidence · 文化证据

Gaitian and the Song of Chile show a Chinese sky-as-cover image.

盖天说与《敕勒歌》表现了中国文化中的“天空如盖”意象。

Yijing classification · 易经分类

Qian is an experimental structural classification, not an etymology.

乾卦属于实验性结构分类，不是词源。

Scientific description · 科学说明

Atmospheric science and ancient cosmology must remain distinct.

现代大气科学与古代宇宙观必须保持区分。

09

Result · 结论

English

天空 is the default lexical translation. 天, 昊, 苍穹 and 穹盖 become preferable when context activates compactness, literary vastness, heaven or the cover metaphor. 盖 alone remains an experimental Collanguage mapping rather than a standard translation.

中文

天空是默认词义翻译；当语境激活简洁表达、文学辽阔感、天界观念或覆盖隐喻时，天、昊、苍穹和穹盖可能更加合适。单独的盖仍属于 Collanguage 实验映射，而不是标准翻译。
