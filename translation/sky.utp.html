03

Semantic Protocol Analysis · 语义协议分析

The protocol does not replace dictionary meaning. It identifies the relations activated by a particular sentence. · 协议并不取代词典意义，而是识别具体句子所激活的关系。

S1

ABOVE · 上方

Sky is located above the observer and organizes vertical orientation.

天空位于观察者上方，组织了垂直方向关系。

S2

SPACE · 空间

Sky presents an open visual field in which distance and movement become perceptible.

天空呈现一个开放的视觉场，使距离与运动变得可感知。

S3

COVER · 覆盖

From the ground, the sky may be imagined as a canopy extending over the world.

从地面观察时，天空可以被想象为覆盖世界的穹盖。

S4

BOUNDARY · 边界

The visible sky can appear to enclose the human world while also opening toward the unknown.

可见的天空既像人类世界的边界，也向未知敞开。

05

Mapping Justification · 映射理由

English

Semantic relation: a cover extends above something and establishes an upper boundary. In Gaitian imagery, heaven resembles a rounded canopy covering the earth.

Phonetic relation: sky /skaɪ/ and gài /kaɪ˥˩/ share an /aɪ/-like sequence. Within the Collanguage consonant-family hypothesis, /k/ and /g/ may be treated as related velar stops.

Unmatched features: the initial /s/, voicing difference, syllable structure, and Mandarin tone remain unmatched. Therefore the comparison is a candidate mapping, not proof of cognacy.

中文

语义关系：盖位于某物之上并形成上方边界。在盖天意象中，天如圆形穹盖覆盖大地。

语音关系：sky /skaɪ/ 与 gài /kaɪ˥˩/ 具有近似 /aɪ/ 的音段。在 Collanguage 辅音家族假说中，/k/ 与 /g/ 可以作为相关的软腭塞音比较。

未对应部分：开头的 /s/、清浊差别、音节结构和汉语声调都没有完全对应。因此它属于候选映射，不是同源证明。

06

Yijing Structural Classification · 易经结构分类

English

Candidate structure: Qian 乾. Sky, upward orientation, continuous movement and creativity make Qian a structural candidate for this case.

The classification describes a pattern of state and relation. It does not claim that the English word sky, or the Chinese words 天、昊 and 盖, historically originated from the hexagram.

中文

候选结构：乾卦。天、向上、持续运行与创造性，使乾卦成为本案例的结构候选。

这种分类描述的是状态与关系模式，并不声称英语 sky 或汉语“天、昊、盖”在历史上来源于该卦象。

Classification dimensions · 分类维度：
State · 状态：open / active · 开放／活跃； Position · 位置：above · 上方； Movement · 运动：continuous · 运行不息； Polarity · 极性：yang candidate · 阳性候选； Transformation · 转化：creative emergence · 创生。

09

Evaluation Result · 评测结论

A

Everyday translation · 日常翻译

Use 天空 for a neutral reference to the visible sky.

中性表达可见天空时，优先使用天空。

B+

Literary translation · 文学翻译

Use 天、苍天、苍穹 or 昊天 when register and imagery justify them.

当语体和意象需要时，可以使用天、苍天、苍穹或昊天。

EXP

Collanguage mapping · 共通语言映射

Keep sky ↔ 盖 as an experimental phonetic–semantic mapping requiring further cases and counterexamples.

将 sky ↔ 盖 保留为实验性语音—语义映射，继续收集支持案例与反例。

乾

Structural classification · 结构分类

Qian is a candidate for upward, active, continuous and creative sky imagery.

对于向上、活跃、持续运行和创造性的天空意象，乾卦可作为候选结构。
